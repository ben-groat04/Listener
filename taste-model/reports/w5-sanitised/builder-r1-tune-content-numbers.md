## 2. Honest tuning (RAN: fast TUNE, evalkit.inner, choices logged in choices.jsonl)
Procedure: at each outer cutoff, score every recipe of the grid on inner folds built from the fold's own train (pooled inner sauc, 24 recipes for wide grid, 14 narrow), pick best, refit on whole train, score outer fold. Inner folds with fewer than 2000 earlier train streams are dropped (needed: the first outer cutoffs produce inner folds before the data starts; an unguarded run crashed in song_table: failed attempt, fixed in tuned.py). ## 5. Selection / variants accounting
Variants scored on TUNE: 91 recipes (incl. default), 5 tuned procedures + 1 walk-forward composite, final = one of the 5 tuned procedures chosen after seeing TUNE (chosen as the least harmful, tunedA_m). Selection could inflate the final's TUNE score by at most the spread among the 5 procedures (0.598-0.625, best 0.625 = the reported one), so the reported 0.625 is the BEST of five tuned procedures; the typical honest tuned procedure is 0.61. The fixed default's 0.632 is itself the result of earlier TUNE-based tuning (history) and is selection-inflated by an unknown amount; the random-joint recipes (never optimised) average 0.585, which indicates how much of the default's level is due to prior selection plus real signal. The G_TUNE and G_NARROW grids were designed after seeing the OAT results (G_NARROW removes obviously harmful options), a further small leak toward favourable tuning that nevertheless did not help.

## 6. Strongest case against my result
(a) TUNE is the era on which the default was tuned by earlier workflows, so "no recipe beats the default" is partly "the default was selected on this exact data": the grid cannot show a real improvement that earlier selection already absorbed, and the failure of inner tuning could be partly because the defaults are TUNE-lucky. (b) With only 1074 tried songs and about 4-8 inner folds of 20-100 candidates, the inner fold noise (sd of pooled inner AUC ~0.03) swamps differences of 0.01 between recipes; a larger or smoother inner design (e.g. many more folds, shrinkage towards the default) was not tried beyond the margin rule. (c) The grid is about axes the earlier agents also tuned; it does not cover essentia blocks or a different model family. (d) The calibration with permuted labels gives null spreads much larger than near-default neighbours' real spread, so "within chance" is a weak statement for those recipes; the bootstrap intervals are what is informative. (e) Final selected among five procedures after seeing TUNE; LATE/HOLDOUT are partly used and are confirmation only.
