Dropping stub groups (min 30 songs) hurts (-0.008).
- The one change with a consistent sign across all six fold groupings is using the newest songs with partial-horizon (rate scaled) targets instead of hiding the newest 90 days (mat0_scale, +0.006 TUNE; +0.006 / +0.001 / +0.008 / +0.011 / +0.011 on tune_q1/q2/m/h/y). - Freshness of the listening record matters a lot (cutting back the record by 30 / 90 / 180 days costs -0.010 / -0.040 / -0.079 sauc); how old data is down-weighted does not (half-lives and windows are neutral to negative, never chosen by the tuner).

## 4. Strongest case against my result
1. The gain of the declared final over the reference is +0.006 on TUNE (80% includes 0); the held-out slices (numbers removed) do not change that: statistically indistinguishable from nothing, and I chose it AFTER viewing 87 variants; the chance best-of-87 would be several times larger (+0.02-0.03). Per fold it wins 5 of 10 folds. I declared it for sign consistency, which is weak evidence because the alternative fold sets re-use the same songs.
2. The sensitivity map is anchored on a default that was itself tuned on TUNE in earlier workflows (winner's curse), so "all neighbours are worse" is biased; the OAT and random configs cannot be read as the cost of detuning. A truly neutral map would need a fresh slice, which does not exist.
3. The conclusion "tuning does not help" is conditional on this grid, on 2-4 inner windows of 3 months (very noisy: 100-300 songs each), on the early cutoffs having 0-2 inner folds, and on TUNE being dominated by two quarters (2023-Q1, 2023-Q4 carry about 69% of the pair weight, lead's number). A procedure with better inner design (more windows, repeated, a smoother selection such as averaging top configs) was not tried and could do better; the margin variants show shrinkage helps.
4. The staleness numbers cut the whole record, not just the model refresh; they do not isolate retraining frequency. I did not test the PCA/learner interaction with signal choices (kept at base values by the brief), and the PCA is reused across inner folds.
5. The chance calibration is bootstrap-based (sampling noise only); it does not include variability from refitting the model on different label noise (random-label refits were not done).
