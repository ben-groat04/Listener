## 3. Missing-audio policy (ran; dev data only; results/missing_analysis.txt, analyse_missing.py)
- Who: among the 1074 TUNE tried rows, 24% lack at least one audio clip (240 have no preview URL at all, 21 have a URL but no clip). They are 261 distinct songs by 93 artists (the 3 artists with most missing songs hold 32%). Love rate: audio 0.298, no URL 0.375, URL-but-no-clip 0.476. They are lower in popularity (mean 13.6 vs 38.7; popularity 0 in 38% vs 25%), shorter plays history before (exposure 2.1 vs 3.3), and similar in new-artist share (33% vs 36%).
- Not stable over time: love rate missing vs audio: 2021 0.62 vs 0.67, 2022 0.47 vs 0.48, 2023 0.32 vs 0.22 (the whole gap is 2023). By exposure bucket the gap is 0.00 (0), +0.08 (1-2), +0.09 (3-5), +0.27 (6+; 6+ bucket has few missing). New-artist rows: 0.29 vs 0.22; known-artist rows: 0.43 vs 0.34.
- Gain of ranking missing songs high (essentia fill high), paired vs default: sauc +0.0192 [+0.0011,+0.0381]; within-artist (wa) -0.0020 [-0.039,+0.028]; within-exposure-bucket (wx) +0.0090 [-0.0085,+0.0289]; MERT fill high only: +0.0062 sauc, wa -0.0102, wx +0.0004. On audio-present rows the policy has no effect by construction (0.0000); on new-artist rows +0.038 [+0.007,+0.074], known-artist rows +0.013 [-0.010,+0.031]; excluding the 3 artists with most missing songs +0.032 [+0.010,+0.057] (so not driven by a few artists). By fold: mean +0.0146 [-0.0026,+0.0318], Q 8.1/8, I2 0.01, 9 usable folds, per-fold deltas range -0.086..+0.110.
- Missing indicator alone: sauc 0.5445 [0.519,0.570], wa 0.5453 [0.526,0.570], wx 0.532 [0.509,0.554]: a weak positive association that also exists within artist (pairs of songs of the same artist) and within exposure bucket, i.e. it is not only an artist composition effect, but the effect size is small (about +0.04 over chance) and its time stability is poor (2023 only).
- Would such songs exist as candidates? In the harness open_love table (TUNE, tried songs vs decoys) the has_preview baseline is exactly 0.500 for all/new/known, i.e. constant over the compared rows (the decoys all have previews as far as that table shows); in the open_love_full table it is 0.455 (below chance, the tried-song effect again). I did not inspect the decoy universe (forbidden), so 'decoys never lack audio' is inferred from that constant score, not verified. Only in the tried-pool comparison (songs the user actually played) can the lead show up; in a search among unplayed songs, a missing-audio boost has nothing to act on, or, if applied to a catalogue that includes preview-less songs, would reward catalogue availability (popularity 0 / no preview is more common among the missing).
- Decision: NOT part of a model meant for candidate search. Reasons (belief, supported by the above): (a) the effect is conditional on the song having been played at all (the tried set), and "no preview" is a property of the catalogue entry, not of the music, so it cannot generalise to the open catalogue where all candidates are scored on audio; (b) the gain is concentrated in 2023, undecided at the harness's 80% rule, and absent within-artist in the policy gain; (c) 38% of the missing songs have popularity 0, consistent with unavailable/removed catalogue entries that the user nevertheless kept playing, a data-collection artefact. I cannot prove artefact vs taste from dev data; the evidence leans artefact for search purposes. The final predictor keeps the recipe's neutral fill.
- Below-chance investigation: harness has_preview baseline sauc 0.476 (<0.5, CI 0.445-0.497) on TUNE (held-out value removed), popularity baseline 0.476: has_preview <0.5 is exactly the missing-songs-loved-more effect above (not a bug); popularity 0.476 is the known popularity-is-uninformative/negative baseline (not part of this work). No other score of mine is below 0.5 except the random-label refits (null sauc of default 0.46-0.55, expected).

## Strongest case against my result
1. The central claim (numbers don't matter, tuning doesn't help) rests on 1074 tried songs in 10 folds; all 82 variants are UNDECIDED or NEG at 80%, which also means the grid has little power: a true +0.005 improvement would not show as POS. "No evidence of benefit" is not "no benefit". Typical 80% half-widths are 0.003-0.006 for small moves.
2. Only the neighbourhood of the default was explored, with the training target and time numbers fixed (assigned to another task); a better recipe may be far away.
3. The random-label calibration likely overstates chance (random-target models vary much more than real ones), so it cannot by itself show that real deltas are noise; it shows they are smaller than a lax upper bound.
4. The missing-audio gain (+0.019, lo80 +0.001) is the largest positive in the grid and I am recommending against using it based on reasoning about candidate search and 2023-only concentration, not a decisive dev test; the within-artist missing indicator is positive (+0.045). It could partly be taste (songs without previews are older/niche) and a search that includes preview-less songs could use it.
5. The final is an average whose point estimate is -0.001 below the fixed default; it is declared for robustness and to avoid the fingerprint refusal of an exact copy, not because it is better.
6. The nested-selection family excluded the fill variants and used 4 inner folds of 3-month windows that contain few loved songs (inner folds rank recipes only weakly), so negative results for selection are specific to this inner design.
