## 6. Staleness: model vs record (secondary; the 9 variants of PREREG)
Important limitation: this recipe's listening record enters only as TRAINING TARGETS (audio-only features, PCA is unsupervised), so there are no separate 'history and panel features' and the suggested 'stale model with fresh features' cannot be built literally. I split by signal instead: the new-song percentile targets (population of songs with mature 90-day windows; ridge members newE/newW32) versus the recent-listening panel targets (last K=2 periods of 91 days; members panE/panW); the audio features and PCA are the same in all.
sauc delta vs default (80%): cut back by D = 30 / 90 / 180 days: only new-song signal -0.000 [-.004,+.004] / -.007 [-.014,+.000] / -.012 [-.023,+.002]; only panel signal -.011 [-.021,+.003] / -.031 [-.044,-.017] / -.058 [-.073,-.039]; both -.010 [-.022,+.004] / -.040 [-.054,-.023] / -.079 [-.099,-.050]. Effects are about additive (90 d: -.031 + -.007 vs -.040). Answer: the freshness effect is carried mainly by the recent-listening (panel) record, 4-5x more than by the new-song signal; fresher new-song training adds little (consistent with the maturity family giving only +0.00x). A fit-time vs feature-time split is impossible here (no listening features).

## 9. Strongest case against
(1) Primary UNDECIDED; every positive reading depends on a family and a recipe tuned on these same TUNE songs. (2) Best-of-20 equals chance in the null refits. (3) Leave-one-fold-out collapses the primary to +0.002 without 2023-01; fold-set choice flips the decision. (4) The 'all 20 positive' pattern is expected if the 20 configs are near-copies sharing the same default-vs-anything shift (e.g. ensembling-like smoothing, or default sitting at a mildly bad point of grouping 'quarter'); it is not 20 independent confirmations, and my bootstrap P (0.02) ignores the round-1 selection. (5) The round-1 monotone maturity dose-response is not reproduced. (6) The null calibration used 10 seeds, a noisy null. (7) Staleness decomposition is by signal, not by model vs features.
