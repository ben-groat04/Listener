## 2. Where the builder's "works" statements are weakest (attacks)

1. The declared final's gain is not separated from selection. mat0_scale was picked after 87+4 variants; the sign rule used to pick it is not unique (grp_all, grp_roll182 pass it too) and the alt fold sets re-use the same 1074 songs (same labels, same dominant folds 2023-01 and 2023-10), so they are not replications. The TUNE gain depends on one fold (2023-01).
2. mat0_scale is worse than a neighbour on the evidence the builder itself had: mat60_scale has TUNE lo80 > 0 and is positive in 8 of 10 folds; mat0_scale is 4 of 10. The choice trades fold-robustness for a bigger point gain on coarse fold sets (h, y: +0.011), where the model is staler. That is a hypothesis (fresher info matters), not tested.
3. Post-hoc combos (mat0_scale + one grouping change) reach +0.010 to +0.011 on TUNE and +0.018 on tune_y, but are not reported with numbers in the builder's report body; they were counted as variants but not shown. Not declared (reasonable), but the "none beats default by more than +0.007" headline applies only to the 87-config scope.
4. The chance calibration used in the report is built from all 87 configs and so is inflated by obviously different, high-noise configs; the right benchmark for small tweaks is about +0.005 to +0.006 (see claim 2). This makes the "gain = chance" reading correct but not "chance is several times larger".
5. The default is itself TUNE-tuned (builder says so), so "every neighbour is worse" is partly an artefact; unchanged by my work.
6. Held-out: numbers removed in this copy; neither held-out slice is clean.
7. Nested tuning negative result is conditional on its inner design (2 to 4 noisy inner folds, 45-config grid, pooled sauc argmax); only a few geometries and one aggregation were tried, so "tuning does not help" is a statement about this procedure.
