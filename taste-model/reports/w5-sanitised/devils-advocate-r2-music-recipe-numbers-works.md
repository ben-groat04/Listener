## Main attacks that do not change a verdict but should limit use of the result
1. The default is the Workflow 4 winner, chosen on these same TUNE folds. Every variant is compared with a point that was optimised on the evaluation data, so mean variant delta -0.0024 is what regression to the mean predicts; "0 POS" says little about whether the numbers matter on new data. The builder does not state this.
2. avg81 and default are almost the same ranking (Spearman 0.992-0.998 per fold). The family spans one grid step around the default, so a tie is near-automatic; claim 7 is not evidence of robustness to the numbers, only that averaging neighbours leaves the ranking unchanged. The final is a near-copy of the reference and is declared only to avoid the fingerprint refusal (builder says so).
3. The one POS-leaning lead (fill high, lo80 +0.001) is a handling of songs without previews, i.e. a catalogue property, estimated on 261 rows from 93 artists. Claim 13's supporting reasoning is wrong (above), but the within-artist wa delta (-0.002) and I2 0.01 do not support a real effect either.
4. The nested-selection test is run at only 5 effective cutoffs with 4 inner 3-month folds and very few positives (guard), and the selection family excludes the one move that matters (fill). The negative selection result is therefore specific and low-powered.
5. decision80 in the builder's grid uses nboot 300 with the dump bootstrap, not the harness's FILE_DELTAS80 (1000, fixed seed). I reproduced the same decisions; no 74/8 category flips because the nearest boundary cases (e.g. oat_wm_0 hi80 -0.0016, oat_fill_2 lo80 +0.001) are far from the +-0.005 cut.
6. Variants were also all scored on the 1074 TUNE rows only; no LATE/other-data check of any variant other than the final. Selection accounting is fine because nothing was selected.
