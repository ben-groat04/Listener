## Further findings
- Effort/time: TIMING.txt 22:12:21 to 23:13:23 (61 min) against a 90-min cap; calibration was cut at 10 of 12 planned seeds "for time" although the cap had ~30 min spare and the later null runs took 78 s and 6.4 min. Calibration claims therefore rest on 10 noisy seeds; reported honestly.
- PREREG.md last-modified 22:21:16, after the 35-config run (finished 22:17:53), because the final was appended; its first-write time cannot be verified from the files, so "pre-registered before scoring" is unverifiable from mtimes (it was likely written between 22:13 and 22:17).
- PREREG states the primary rule as "POS if lo80 > 0", the harness as lo80 > 0.005; the report used the harness rule. Both give UNDECIDED for the primary (lo80 about -0.001) but they differ for avg21/avg_mat.
- Family edges: the best cells are at the grid edges (mat 0, grouping "all"); intermediate groupings (half-year, year) and the other signal numbers were not explored, so "lead (a) is null" really means "null for this 3-parameter family tuned on the same songs in round 1".
- Dump column first_play_date, fold_set 'tune' label, --best-ref refusals, holdout refusal and the v5.1 selftest all verified; FILE_DELTAS80 is oriented model_a minus model_b with default as model_a in the pair table (easy to misread as negative deltas). Engineer interpretation of "fix the reference for FILE_DELTAS80" as extra rows is a mild deviation, disclosed.
- Recommendation by the builder ("keep the default") sits uneasily with its own numbers: the same-sign LATE point delta, positive lo80 for avg21 and avg_mat, family-mean P=0.02. A defensible reading under the 80% rule is "small positive shift of about +0.004 to +0.008, selection-inflated, not confirmed".

## Strongest remaining case against the builder's dismissal
(1) "None POS" is produced by the 0.005 buffer; (2) "within chance" mixes a noisy refit null with a bootstrap null whose own P (0.17, 0.02) points the other way at 80%; (3) the monotone-maturity non-replication is untested; (4) the fold-leave-out is a one-fold, post-hoc choice.
