# Song suggestions 1 (2026-11-23)

Made with model `avg21_dev` (w6r1-content-ranker-final, unchanged, no retuning): each song is scored from MERT features of its 30 s preview against a taste profile trained on the owner's full listening history through 2026-11-23 (cutoff = today, all streams). Candidates: Deezer related-artist walk (2 hops from the owner's top 49 artists by plays in the last 12 months; top tracks of the 260 best-connected related artists plus the seed artists' own top tracks) and Deezer genre charts (rock, alternative, pop; 100 tracks each; singer-songwriter genre not matched). 2,821 tracks with a preview were collected; 781 were removed as already played (normalised title + primary artist; Deezer ids cannot be matched to the history's ids), leaving 2,040 candidates, of which 2040 were scored (902 by artists never played). Scores are shown as percentile among scored candidates. Shortcuts: hop 2 was run from all 573 hop-1 artists; at most 8 top tracks per walk artist; played-matching is by name only (title with brackets/remaster suffixes stripped + artist; 3.4% of plays have no artist name in the metadata and were matched by title only; a remix or differently titled version could still slip through; checked by hand for all 30 listed songs); Spotify links not looked up. No tuning or sealed data used.
