# DO NOT CLAIM (standing list for anyone reading frames)

Applies to every video. These are readings that 1 fps frames, small print and research notes do not support. If you think you see one of them, write what is literally visible instead and tag the rest SPECULATED.

1. **Fur** (collars, hats, coats): texture at this resolution does not separate fur from wool or knit. Say "pale, fuzzy collar".
2. **Hand-lettering**: capitals on a board or sign may be painted, stencilled or printed. Describe the letters (colour, case, ground); leave the manner undecided.
3. **Sex or gender of a person seen at 1 fps** (distant, bundled up, from behind): do not state it. Describe clothing and posture only.
4. **Role of men in caps or uniforms** (official, helper, marshal, police, spectator): not readable from a cap. Say "man in a peaked cap, role unclear".
5. **A pole read as a flag band**: a mast, post or wire crossing a flag can look like a dark stripe. Count flag bands only when the cloth itself is clear at full size.
6. **Sub-second timing from 1 fps frames**: a frame labelled `f_NNNN` is the second NNNN-1 give or take about 0.5 s. Cut times, hits and gestures are good to the second only; anything finer needs a frame pulled at an exact time or the detector's cut list.
7. **"Nobody smiles", "nobody crosses a line", "no one looks at the camera"**: sampling one frame per second cannot show an absence. Write "in the frames opened, none shows ...".
8. **Print completed from context**: a name, number or word that is not legible at full size stays "unread", even when context.md or research suggests what it should say.
9. **Gestures and expressions at distance** (raised fist, wave, smile, look to camera): record the literal posture first; read the meaning separately and tag it INFERRED or SPECULATED.
10. **Anything that comes from context.md rather than pixels**: allowed, but tag it as from context and never use it to support a visual claim.
