## What changed between rounds

Only the scores were recorded, not the edits, so the notes below are limited to what the numbers show.

- Round 1 to 2: our flag rate fell from 72% to 61%. video2-text (2/3 to 0/3), video2-forum (3/3 to 2/3) and video1-forum (3/3 to 2/3) improved; video2-review (2/3 to 3/3) and video1-text (0/3 to 1/3) got worse. Real pieces went 4% to 0%.
- Round 2 to 3: a further drop to 44%. video2-forum went 2/3 to 0/3, video2-review 3/3 to 2/3, video1-forum 2/3 to 3/3 (worse), video1-text 1/3 to 0/3. Real pieces rose to 6%.
- Overall: 72% to 44% over three rounds, with real pieces staying low (0 to 6%).

Readings: the two reviews and the video1 forum post stayed the most detectable (video1-review 3/3 in every round). The texts and the video2 forum post became hard to flag. With three judges per piece, one-judge swings are noisy. The final pieces are saved in analysis/music/<video>/MUSIC_VOICES.md.
