## What changed between rounds

Per-piece flag counts are the data above. The run only recorded the scores, not the edits, so the notes below are limited to what the numbers show.

- Round 1 to 2: our flag rate barely moved (72% to 67%). video2-text improved (1/3 to 0/3) and video1-forum improved (3/3 to 1/3), but video1-text got worse (0/3 to 2/3). Real pieces stayed near zero.
- Round 2 to 3: the big drop (67% to 39%). video1-review went 3/3 to 0/3, video2-forum 3/3 to 1/3, video2-review 3/3 to 2/3. video1-forum got worse (1/3 to 3/3). Real pieces rose slightly to 4%.
- Round 3 to 4: overall flat at 39%. video2-review went back up to 3/3 and video2-forum to 2/3; video1-text and video1-forum moved to 1/3, and video1-review stayed at 0/3. Real pieces fell to 0%.

Readings: the video2 forum post and review stayed the most detectable throughout (never below 1/3 and 2/3). The video1 review and the texts were the easiest to pass. Round-to-round swings of one or two judges on single pieces are small samples (3 judges each), so treat individual changes as noisy.
