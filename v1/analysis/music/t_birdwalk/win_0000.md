- Loop regularity: I did not do a bar-by-bar waveform correlation, so I do not claim loop identity (measured: only the uniform hit level and spacing above).
- Overall judgement: reads as a modern-era (digital, hard-limited, hip hop) production with a band-limited, lo-fi mix. INFERRED, medium.

## 9. What a listener feels, and why

- Anticipation and looseness in 0:00-0:12: the near-empty bass (stem at -70 dB), a single hit every 1.56 s and a speech-like voice produce a spacious, teasing, almost announcement feel (voice loud, little behind it).
- Playfulness: CLAP "playful" 0.97-1.0 at 5-20 s (weak evidence), consistent with a chant repeated phrase and a bouncing speech rhythm.
- Slight unease or tension from 0:09-0:12: the downward-sweeping harmonics in the voice (glide) and the stalled chord (A/Am flips) suggest a building lift before the drop; the 0:12.1 arrival gives release: +50 dB bass, +4.9 dB mix, drum density up about 3-8 times.
- Drive from 0:12: syncopated bass at about 0.4-1.1 s spacing and drums at the 152 BPM pulse; narrow centred stereo (0.2-0.3) gives a punchy, focused weight rather than width.
- Lo-fi grit: the 6 kHz ceiling removes air and cymbal sheen, so the whole thing sounds dull-edged and warm or boxy (measured cause: shelf at 5-6 kHz).
