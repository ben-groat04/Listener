## 8. How the window moves the song on

## 9. What a listener feels

- Weight and drive from 0:20: the bass stem is the loudest layer at -14 dBFS and is never off, with the mix at -12 dBFS and 0 dBFS peaks. A body-heavy, loud feeling.
- Lift at 0:24: the voice rises about 1.5 octaves (110-130 Hz to about 290 Hz) and the stereo image collapses to centre (0.24 to 0.07). The roll before it (5 hits per second for 1.75 s) is the anticipation. Sounds like the hook begins.

Not much harmonic motion (A pedal), so the feeling is rhythm and attitude, not emotion from changing chords. CLAP's "playful" (0.5-1.0) agrees, weak evidence.
- Spaciousness is limited: nearly mono and a 6 kHz ceiling make the sound dense and close, not airy.
