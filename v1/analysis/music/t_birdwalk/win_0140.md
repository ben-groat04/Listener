## 8. How this window moves the song on

So the 2:11.4 to 2:23.4 passage is a 12 s loud island between two stripped sections, and the song is alternating "stripped" and "full" in 12 s blocks (1:59 to 2:11.4 stripped, 2:11.4 to 2:23.4 full, 2:23.4 to 2:35.7 stripped, 2:35.7 on full; the lengths are 12.4, 12.0, 12.3 s). ## 9. What a listener feels, and why
- 2:20 to 2:23.4: a pumping, heavy groove that is dense at the end; causes: bass A1 and drum hits at -9 to -16 dB envelope, mix about -13 dBFS with 78 per cent of energy under 150 Hz and the drum roll speeding to 0.20 s intervals (the eighth-note roll at 152 BPM).
- 2:23.4: a sudden drop-out, the floor going from under you; causes: bass at -45 dB, drums at -23 dB, mix -8.5 dB in about 1 s; the voice carries on at the same level (-20 to -21 dB) so the effect is a hole under the voice, not silence.
- 2:24 to 2:35: exposed, bare, a bit restless and playful; causes: the voice is the loudest layer at -18 to -22 dB with nothing under it, the stab layer flickers (-30 to -53 dB, one onset every 0.37 s), the image is wider (S/M -5.1 dB versus -10.8 dB) and brighter (centroid 558 Hz versus 214 Hz). The repeated phrase ("watch me do that bird walk", INFERRED) turns into a chant. Playful is carried by CLAP (playful 0.97 to 0.999 in these windows; weak) and by the bouncing syllable rate (3.5 to 5 per second).
- 2:32.5 to 2:35.7: anticipation; cause: the mix climbs 2 dB and the faint descending streak (visual, unconfirmed) runs into the return, the same device as before the 2:11.4 return, so it is a learned cue.
- 2:35.7: release, weight in the chest; causes: A1 bass note arrives (+58 dB in the stem, mix +4.9 dB), the image narrows (-10.9 dB S/M) and the low band returns to 78 per cent of the energy. 2:36.4: the drums join, with a lift of +1.8 dB.
- Overall feeling: a boastful, loose-limbed hip-hop strut that keeps pulling its floor away and putting it back; the repetition of this on/off alternation (three blocks of about 12 s) makes the drops feel anticipated by the second time.
