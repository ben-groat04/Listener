## A forum post

Elfstedentocht Part 1, Public Service Broadcasting. A mate put it on a playlist and I've had it on way too much since. I'd always filed them under "the sample band" so I wasn't expecting it to be this heavy. Drums right from the start, big fuzzy guitar over the top, and a Dutch bloke reading out a date and then later what sounds like a list of towns. Don't speak Dutch so that's as far as I got.

Bass vanishes for a good while around 1:40 (I think, haven't timed it) and when it comes back it properly lands. The quiet bit after has these deep slow thumps that don't exist on my phone, I honestly thought it was skipping. Stops dead at the end which annoyed me a bit. Anyone heard Part 2?

## A short review

Public Service Broadcasting, Elfstedentocht Part 1. Been on repeat for a few days. It was made for the 50th anniversary of the 1963 Elfstedentocht, the big Dutch ice skating race, and a Dutch voice reads out the date, how cold it was (sixteen below, I think) and later the towns on the route, all over loud drums and distorted guitars, probably guitars anyway. No singing and no real tune.

Best part for me is when the bass cuts out a bit over a minute in and everything goes thin and high for what feels like ages, then the bass comes back and it hits properly hard. Second best is the quiet bit later with the slow low thumps, which I only noticed after swapping my speakers for headphones, on the laptop it just sounds like a gap. That's my gripe really, half of it is missing if you listen on the wrong thing.

The last stretch is faster and brighter, loads of cymbals, and then it just stops dead, which is a bit of a letdown. Quite short too, 3:09.
