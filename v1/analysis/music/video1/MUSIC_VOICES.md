## A short review

Elfstedentocht Part 1 is Public Service Broadcasting's piece for the 50th anniversary of the 1963 Elfstedentocht, the big Dutch ice skating race, and it's a lot heavier than I expected from them (I'd always filed them under the samples band). No singing and no chorus, just drums going the whole way through, a huge fuzzy layer of what's either guitars or synths, and a Dutch voice reading out the date, sixteen degrees below zero, the distance and later the towns on the route. Best bit is where the bass goes missing about a minute and a quarter in, so everything turns thin and a bit tinny for what feels like ages, then it comes crashing back in and honestly that's the whole reason to play it. The quiet stretch after that is just a few very low slow thumps, which I got nothing from on my laptop, so use proper speakers or headphones. The last part is faster with loads of cymbals. Then it stops dead at 3:09, which I thought was a bit rubbish.
