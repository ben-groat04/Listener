## 2. Answers to questions put to me
- **Form: climax at 1:27, 1:40 or 2:48; held breath or gasp?** Settled in point 3 and 4 above. 1:27 is a lift (loudness and width return before the bass; +2.4 dB, width +0.30 in F2), a preparation, not the peak.
- **Rhythm: where does the sub fall?** Answered in point 1. Consequence for feeling: the six drops at 6.15 s spacing (8 beats; two bars at 78, four at 156) read as a slow regular gesture with a slight forward lean, which supports "waiting for something large" more than "dread". Playback caveat stands (R3-sound endorses it): on small speakers the 42 Hz thump largely disappears and the felt event is the drop in the `other` layer (-5 dB) and the narrowing.
- **Voice: does the narration quicken?** No (point 2).
- **Sound: what makes the blocks feel as they do?** R3-sound: the 2:00 to 2:14 block feels small because it is high, thin and narrow (6 LU down, mono); the final run feels hard because it is loudest, steady (about 0.3 LU range) and has a hat/crash share about four times larger. - **Lineage: sampled or played kit, stems muted?** I cannot hear the difference, but I note for feeling: R3-sound finds the bass falls to a flat floor of about -65 dB at the edits, which behaves like a muted stem. - **Harmony: yearning from what?** R3-harmony: the only melodic yearning is the stepwise line (A, C, B, G, D) over the missing tonic at 1:15 to 1:40. I agree and add R3-sound's weak finding that the line swells slowly (median rise to peak about 0.22 s, low confidence), which would make it feel sustained and breathing rather than plucked.

## 3. Moment-by-moment journey (settled; section boundaries from R3-form)
Every line: what I expect an attentive listener to feel (interpretation) and the measured cause.
- **0:01 to 0:10, pickup and build.** Curious, leaning in; a pulse starts and layers join. Cause: drum comb and layers entering (IMG 0000, LV). Medium.
- **0:10 to 0:32, narration over the band.** Informed, steady, a little detached: a calm voice telling you facts over a pulse that never relaxes. Cause: flat narration, constant 4 to 5 peaks per second [R3-voice], drums and bass continuous. Medium-high.
- **0:32 to 0:41, band alone.** A relief and a first surge of "now it is ours": the narration leaves (about 0:31.7, beat-aligned) and the music is exposed. Cause: voice exit, mix-neutral but speech is a foreground signal. Medium.
- **0:41 to 1:15, the build.** Determination, forward drive, momentum accumulating by repetition and ramping loudness (LV, loudest ramp to about -6.6 LUFS at 1:07 [R3-form]). Not a melodic climb: the chords sit on a D pedal. Medium-high.
- **1:15 to 1:27, bass-less float.** The floor falls away: light, suspended, slightly uneasy. Cause: bass -36 dB, `other` -5.4 dB, tonic D nearly absent from the upper layer (R2-harmony: D 0.08 to 0.26). High on cause, medium on the word.
- **1:27 to 1:40, the lift.** Lifting, opening out, yearning: width +0.30, `other` +7.6 dB, a stepwise line over no floor. Still no bass, so it feels like rising without ground. Medium-high.
- **1:40 to 1:55, arrival.** The emotional high point of the first half: the bass slams back after 24 s of absence, with the width pulled into the centre. Weight, not loudness (point 5). Medium-high on cause, medium on the ranking.
- **1:55 to 2:14, pull-back.** A sudden emptying: bass out again (-35 dB), mix -3 dB, then a thin, high, mono, hollow block (`other` centroid about 1480 Hz [R2-sound]). Held breath, exposed. Medium.
- **2:00 to 2:38, the slow thumps and the town list.** A slow heavy gesture every 6.15 s over a fast flutter; anticipation, "setting off", and from about 2:15 an itinerary being read. Anticipation tipping toward mild dread at most. Medium.
- **2:38 to 2:47, bass locks on.** Weight returns, the sense of resuming a journey. Cause: bass +42.8 dB. Medium-high.
- **2:47.8 to 2:48.2, the held breath.** Then release into a brighter, wider, harder run. Medium.
- **2:48 to 3:08.4, the sprint.** Hard, driven, loudest and steadiest; grinding from chromatic friction and bright cymbal-led drums (high-frequency drum share 3.3 to 12.4 percent [R2-sound]), not from a minor mode. Medium-high.
- **3:08.4, the cut.** Abrupt, as if the effort continues offscreen. Whether this is the intended ending or an edit of the video is unknown (see section 4). The missing resolving cadence (R3-form: "no resolving cadence") means the listener is left in momentum, not rest.

## 5. Settled conclusions (5 bullets)
- **The feeling is built by subtraction and addition on a fixed pulse, not by tune or key.** Drums run continuously at about 0.19 s subdivisions with 0.77 s accents (R3-rhythm); harmony stays on a D pedal; the drama comes from bass, wall width and voice entering and leaving. High on mechanism, medium on the feelings.
- **Two peaks: an emotional arrival at 1:40 and a physical release at 2:48 to 3:08.** The 1:40 effect is weight and width (the bass returns after 24 s, +42.6 dB, width -0.27) not loudness (+0.1 LU); 2:48 is loudest, steadiest and brightest. Medium-high.
- **The only yearning is the bass-less stepwise line from 1:15 to 1:40; "stoic" is texture and rhythm, not mode.** The third is at most 0.16 in power (R2-harmony), so "sad" or "triumphant" by mode is unsupported; the grit at 2:49 is chromatic friction plus bright drums. Medium-high.
- **The voice is the neutral narrative clock; it adds no excitement of its own.** Steady pace, no quickening, free against the grid (R3-voice); the 1:46 and 1:57 "voices" are leakage and are removed. The 2:47.8 hole reads as a held breath ending in release. High on the voice facts, medium on the reading.
- **Overall character: "disciplined urgency, heavy step with a fast heartbeat", ending in an abrupt cut.** The 0.77 s weight, 0.38 s walking step and 0.19 s flutter never change gear; layers switch cleanly on a bar grid (bass floor flat at -65 dB). Whether it is played or programmed, and how the cut is meant, are open. Medium.
