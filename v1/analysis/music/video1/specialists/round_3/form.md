## 1. Questions put to me, answered

**Lineage: re-test the bar counts against drum-onset phase, not the tracker; do the sub hits and the 2:38 return share a bar phase? (medium)**
I refitted a sixteenth grid (period 0.7693/4 s) to kick-band (below 150 Hz) drum onsets from 0:30 to 2:45 [mine form_r3]. The fit is tight (mean residual 0.045 of a slot), so the grid is real. But the kick-band slot histogram is nearly flat over the even slots (counts 39 to 45 on slots 0, 4, 8, 12 and most others; weak only on slots 2, 9, 14), so the kick band cannot tell me which beat is "one". I therefore do **not** claim a drum-derived downbeat; that is the honest limit. What the refit does show is phase *consistency*: against the drum grid, the events sit at beat-in-bar 0.4 to 1.15 (voice end 0.24; bass out 1:15 0.91; wall 0.64; bass in 1:40 1.02; bass out 1:55 0.52; the three sub hits 0.58, 0.50, 0.43; 2:38 bass in 0.93; stop 1.15). The three sub hits share one phase (the 0.07 drift per two bars is the period fit). The 2:38 return sits about 0.4 beat later than the subs, but bass events are slow ramps (measured threshold lateness 0.3 to 0.4 beat in R2 section 2.1), so I read them as the same phase. Result: counts of 4, 4, 5, 2, 2, 2, 8, 3 bars stand; they are exact multiples of the grid and not tracker artefacts. The 5 and 3 remain odd; I cannot upgrade them to "2/4/8 only". Lineage may take "bar-locked, loop-built" at medium, not higher, from me.

**Feeling (R2 question): which is the climax, 1:27, 1:40 or 2:48? Held breath or gasp?**
Feeling ranks 1:40 (arrival after absence) first and 2:48 second; I ranked 2:48-3:08 first on level (K-weighted -6.4 to -7.0 LUFS-short), brightness and width. These are not contradictory once separated: **1:40 is the emotional high point of the first half** (weight and width return after the bass-less lift; Harmony's stepwise line and "yearning" [R3-harmony, bullet 3] ends exactly there), and **2:48-3:08 is the physical high point** (loudest, steadiest, brightest, only run with no further change). Gasp or held breath is a human judgement; the measurement is a 0.45 s hole with guitar and the narrator's last syllable inside it [R2-form 2.3], which fits a held breath that ends in release, not a shock.
