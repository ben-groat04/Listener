## 2. Answers to questions put to me

**Form Q2a: is the 0:42-2:50 "wander" a modulation?** No. The bass roots E-G-D-(B) and then B-A-D are all in B Aeolian (B C# D E F# G A); the roots use the 3rd, 4th, 6th, 7th degrees plus the tonic. [AF] 30 s key labels (E minor, G major, D major) are chroma-detector answers to the same pitch set with different emphasis, not key changes. The persistent B (and F#) in [OC] (B 0.26 and F# 0.15 even in the 4:50-4:59 pad; B and F# also top the intro) holds B as pedal. Relative-major lean (D/G emphasis) is real, but it is modal ambiguity inside one pitch set. (M-H.)

**Form Q2b and Feeling: does B major act as V of Em, a "pull" before drops?** Measured third of the B-root bars (other stem, D vs D#, where the major third would be D#): 0:52.5-0:56 D 0.089, D# 0.058; 1:53-1:56 D 0.060, D# 0.080; 2:21-2:24 D 0.089, D# 0.083; 3:00.5-3:04 (loop B) D 0.134, D# 0.058. E is as strong as the third in the loop-A B bars (E 0.10-0.16). **The B bars do not show a clear D# (major third)**; D# is only marginally above D in two of three loop-A bars and is not above the noise floor (A# is 0.05-0.07, the same level, so D# 0.06-0.08 is noise-grade). I cannot call it B major (L-M). What does hold, independent of the third: **bass B moving to bass E (B to Em) is a fifth-down root motion, so it has dominant-to-tonic *feeling* in the bass line even without a leading tone**, and the B bar is the only bass root that is not E, G or D. Feeling's "tension then release in 4 s at 0:52.5-0:56" is therefore reasonable as a *bass-root* pull but not as a V7 chord. The only B bar that has D clearly lit is loop B's (3:00.5), where it is B-D-F# = Bm (H-M).

**Rhythm Q1: do the 0:47.5 "A" and the 4:10.4-4:12.6 "Em flicker" fall on bar lines?** Bass roots show no A in loop A: D begins 0:48.2, so the [CH] "A" at 0:47.5 sits about 0.7 s *before* a chord change (the last beat or so of the E-G-D cycle's final slot, E 0:44.2 then G... in fact G 0:44.2-0:48.2). Both fall in the **second bar of a slot, ahead of the next slot**, i.e. phrase-end approach motion rather than a phrase boundary. (M.)

**Voice Q (via Feeling Q3): which chord do the bursts at 4:40.6, 4:42.6 ... 4:58.6 fall on?** Other-stem chroma for each 1.5 s from the burst starts is almost identical every time: B 0.23-0.28, F# 0.13-0.15, A 0.10-0.12 (and A# noise 0.08). In Bm it is the tonic with an added minor seventh (A), a bare "B power fifth plus A" (H on pitch classes, L on third; D is only 0.08 here, much lower than in the loop B bars at 0.13 to 0.16, so the **third has been dropped in the final minute**). Before 4:49 bass A/D still cycled (A 4:40.8, D 4:44.8) while the pad stayed on B-F#-A, which confirms the pad is a drone and the bass makes the progression (H). That also matches Lineage's "B pedal for minutes".

**Sound Q3: does the intro pad hold one chord, and is the ambiguity a phase artefact?** Mono-sum chroma 0:00-0:08: B 0.15, F# 0.14, G 0.11, D 0.10; L: F# 0.15, B 0.14, G 0.13, D 0.11; R: B 0.19, F# 0.14, G 0.12, D 0.11; side channel: B 0.19, F# 0.16, G 0.13, D 0.12.
