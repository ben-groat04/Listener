## 6. How it moves the song on
- Previous window 4:20-4:40 [img 0260s]: the voice was a sustained mid-band line (w+0-17), the lead sat high, chords moved often, and the bass broke into gated stabs only at w+16.5-19.5 with a gap at w+18.5. In 4:40-5:00 the voice reverts to short bursts, the harmonic travel stops, and the bass stab pattern seen at the end of 0260s becomes the new normal after 4:48.5.
- So the window is **a stripped-back run-in to the ending**: the same drive, but with the bass and pitch movement withdrawn, then a hard stop after 5:00 [img 0300s: two sharp hits at about 5:00.5 and 5:02.5, then silence]. It is subtraction, not build in loudness (the level slips by 2-3 dB) but a build in suspense through removed low end, widening stereo image and the metronomic 2 s voice.

## 7. What a listener feels, and why
- **Relentless drive, then hollowness**: the unchanged fast drums and 160 bpm grid keep urgency, while the bass dropping at 4:48.5 and the mix going 2-3 dB quieter and wider [levels] makes the groove feel suddenly light and exposed. Confidence in the cause: high; feeling: medium.
- **Bleak, fixed**: static B minor for 12 s (4:48-5:00) with no cadence [chords, img chroma] gives a stuck, endurance feeling, as against the more restless 4:20-4:40. Medium.
- **Human, clipped, indistinct voice**: 2-second chopped bursts without words read as a person heard in snatches, impersonal and ritualistic, tied to the regular spacing. Medium.
- **Anticipation of an ending**: the removal of the bass and then the abrupt cut at about 5:01-5:03 is a measurable stop; a listener would feel the dropout as the track running out of strength rather than resolving. The final hit (almost mono, -14 dBFS) is blunt. Medium-high (the stop is a measurement; the "running out" reading is interpretation).
- CLAP (weak evidence): genre moves from "synth-pop / drum machine" to "drum and bass" (4:45-5:00) and mood from "nostalgic" to "playful" at 4:50-5:00 [clap]; I do not trust "playful" or "reggae" (5:00), they look like model artefacts for sparse offbeat bass stabs and fast hats. Low.
