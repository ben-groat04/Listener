## What a viewer feels here, and why (INTERPRETATION)

Foreboding, hush and secrecy. The feeling comes from darkness (most frames are under-lit), the shaky handheld images of a crowd that seems to be waiting, and the newspapers that already tell us it will be superhumanly hard. Holding the scene in near-darkness makes the viewer lean in. The excess of grain and blur makes it feel like a remembered or half-recovered document. The lamp/eyes image (0:12) is unsettling because it looks like a staring face. Against the quiet images the continuous music makes it feel like the start of something inevitable. Faces at 0:15 are the first emotional contact: serious, tense, no festivity.
