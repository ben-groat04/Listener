## 3. Sound in this stretch

Words. The only transcribed speech here is at the very end:
- Transcribe (Dutch, Whisper medium), 1:19.7-1:26.9: "575 wedstrijdrijders, rond 9500 toerrijders, een aantal Zweden, Canadezen, Zwitsers en Dalmatians."
- Translate pass: "575 race drivers. Around 9,500 tour riders, a number of Dutchmen, Canadians, Swiss and Belgians."
My correction (judgement, moderate confidence): "wedstrijdrijders" is "competitive skaters" (race entrants), "toerrijders" is "tour skaters" (the non-competitive recreational class, "tourists"), so "575 competitive skaters, around 9,500 tour skaters". The nationalities list is unclear: the two Whisper passes disagree ("Dalmatians" vs "Belgians"). "Zweden" (Swedes) is likely right in the first, "Dutchmen" in the second looks odd given the Dutch context, so I would not trust either list. Possibly "Zweden, Canadezen, Zwitsers en Belgen". The figures 575 and ~9,500 fit the research note that there were about 9,862 starters (575 + ~9,500 roughly = 10,075, which is close but not identical, so the numbers may be approximate or whisper-corrupted).

## 4. What a viewer feels here, and why

INTERPRETATION. This is the most kinetic stretch so far. The fast cutting, the extreme close-ups of backs and bibs, motion blur, and the camera tipping and rolling at 1:05-1:09 make you feel inside the pack: jostled, anonymous, breathless, with no one individual emerging. The flat, loud, sustained music pushes the same way, a feeling of relentless effort. The cut to the dark silhouettes at 1:13-1:14 gives a short, eerie, lonely dip: the skater has no face, and that reads as endurance and anonymity (and, with hindsight about the race's reputation, hardship). The crowd rows (1:02) are silent and watchful rather than cheering, which keeps the tone sombre rather than festive. Then the music drops, the shots lengthen, and the scene turns to officialdom: pens, tables, flags. The mood moves from raw physical struggle to bureaucratic counting. It feels like the film stepping back to count the cost.
