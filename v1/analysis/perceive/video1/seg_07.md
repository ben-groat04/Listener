## What a viewer feels here, and why

(INTERPRETATION.) After the loud, driving 1:49-1:58 stretch, the film slows into a hushed, intimate passage. Faces fill the frame, everyone is breathing steam or sipping hot drink, and the exhausted skater on the white ice is the single clearest image of what the race costs. The mood is weary, sympathetic and slightly anxious: warmth sought in the cold, with an ambulance parked quietly as a reminder of danger.
