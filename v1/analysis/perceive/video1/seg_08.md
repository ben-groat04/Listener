## What a viewer feels here and why
- OBSERVATION: Quick, signage-driven cutting with a loud, building backing; a flat list read over it.
- INTERPRETATION: A sense of a countdown or gathering momentum, as if the whole province is being called up. The repeated signposts make a place feel real and specific (the sign reads "welcome", "50 km", the inn has a licence), which is quietly comic and affectionate, and gives a homely, municipal face to a huge event. Then the mood turns at 2:37-2:40: the blurred head, the grey wet ice and the line of silhouetted men create a hush and anticipation, which is where the tension of waiting lands. The hoar-frost "Welkom in Workum" and the wet ice shot are the most beautiful moments.
