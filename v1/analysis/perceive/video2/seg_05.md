## 4. What a viewer feels

Cold, bleakness and then gathering excitement. The bleached drifts and tiny far-off walkers feel lonely, almost hostile; the colour drifting through violet to gold gives a dreamlike, remembered quality. The cut to the bridge is a human jolt: a crowd, a lorry, a watching man. The blur and whip-pans in 1:33-1:37 convey a jostling, anticipatory bustle, like a town waking for an event. The windmill resolves it into a calm postcard-like Dutch icon, a held breath before the louder music.
