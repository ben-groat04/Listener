## Shot-by-shot (OBSERVATION)
All footage is colour archival film (consistent with the Fries Film Archief material in context.md; I did not verify provenance from the picture). Grain, soft focus, slight colour cast (cool blue ice, warm yellow-pink highlights), a dark film-gate curl visible at the top of many frames. Handheld, mostly from a following position on the ice.

- **4:00 (tail of shot 65, 238.8-240.6).** Close rear view of two skaters on a smooth blue-grey ice lane between snowbanks. The left skater wears a white woolly hat and a white bib reading "157"; the right wears a dark hat and carries a wooden-looking object low at the waist (likely a skate guard or similar; uncertain). Both lean forward, hands clasped behind the back, the classic long-distance skating posture. Empty flat landscape ahead, a farmhouse in the distance.
- **4:01-4:02 (shot 66 start, 240.6).** Cut to a wide, static-looking shot straight down the ice lane: a group of skaters far away walking or skating towards camera, a crowd of dark-clad spectators massed behind them, a steep-roofed house, a shed, a mast. Low, cool daylight, pale sky.
- **4:03.** Violent whip-pan blur: house and figures smeared sideways. Brightness drops (147 to 115). I read this as a camera swing or a missed-cut transition; the detector did not mark a cut here, so shot 66 as listed (240.6-246.28) seems to contain several pictures.
- **4:04-4:06.** Very close, tilted shots of skaters' backs and shoulders: one in a grey-and-white striped knitted jersey with a bobble hat, another in a dark jacket and dark hat, thrusting past the lens. Ice streaks and snow smear diagonally. At 4:06 a distant skater in red runs along a snowbank, with the striped-jersey skater in the foreground. Handheld, jerky, camera apparently very near the skaters.
- **4:07-4:09 (cut at 246.28).** Wide, low-angle view of a dense knot of skaters on blue-purple ice approaching camera, a pale green girder bridge and bank behind with more onlookers. One man in a red hat in front; another in black, arms hanging, mid-stride. Several faces are turned toward the lens. Cuts here come fast (246.28, 247.12, 248.16, 248.88).
- **4:10 (shot 70, 248.9-250.5).** Close, slightly blurred head-on shot: a young man in a dark fur-collared coat strides at the camera, mouth slightly open, eyes on the lens (the one clear face in the stretch; the face detector registers one at 4:08 and 4:16-4:17). Behind him are others in yellow-and-black and red tops, bridge above. Intense, breathless look.
- **4:11 (shot 71, 250.5-251.8).** Overexposed, golden backlit scene: a skater bent double, one leg extended (apparently stooping, adjusting something, or recovering). Onlookers in long dark coats stand motionless on the left and others on the right, frost-bleached sky. Brightness peaks (172-182) and saturation falls: the picture goes nearly white-gold.
- **4:12-4:14 (shot 72 start, 251.84).** Wide shot from low down, a small bunch of skaters in a line, with spectators and a bare tree, hazy sun glare, long shadows. Soft, pale, nearly monochrome with a warm cast (warmth values go positive, +11 to +13 at 4:11-4:13).
- **4:15-4:19.** Hard cut (not in shots.json) to extreme close, handheld jostling: two skaters shoulder to shoulder. One in a black-and-white zigzag-patterned jersey with a bib partly visible, carrying wooden skate-like objects on the back; the other in a red bobble hat and bib. 4:17 shows back of a leg and a wooden object swinging. 4:18-4:19 are blurred, heavy contact, the red-collared figure thrusting into frame. Saturation is high here (133 at 4:15, the most saturated second of the stretch).

Editing effects: no dissolves, titles or graphics seen. Whip-pan blur at 4:03 and motion blur at 4:18-4:19. Rhythm is alternate wide/close: wide (4:01), tight (4:04-4:06), wide (4:07-4:09), tight (4:10), blown-out wide (4:11-4:14), tight (4:15-4:19).

## People, places, objects, text
- **People:** all appear to be adult men, mostly young to middle-aged, in dark wool jackets, knitted jerseys, bobble hats, caps; skaters wear paper or cloth bibs with numbers. Spectators are in long dark overcoats. Expressions: absorbed, strained, one direct glare to camera at 4:10. No women or children clearly seen.
- **Places:** open Frisian-looking polder under snow, a frozen canal lane cleared and worn smooth, wooden or brick farmhouses, a girder (lift or swing) bridge. This matches the Elfstedentocht per context.md, but nothing visible names a town.
- **Objects:** skates (long blades), wooden carried items, bibs, ice-streaked snowbanks.
- **On-screen text:** bib "157" at 4:00 and 4:01-ish (frame f_0241 clearly shows 157). Other bib digits at 4:15 and 4:19 are partly out of frame or blurred and I will not guess. No captions or subtitles.

## What a viewer feels (INTERPRETATION)
Pressure and relentlessness. The sound holds steady at its loudest while the camera crowds into jostling bodies, so the viewer is inside the pack: breath, shoulders, elbows, effort. The cool blue ice and dark clothing give a hard, wintry edge; the sudden blown-out golden shot at 4:11-4:14 is a visual exhale, almost a mirage, with onlookers standing like silent witnesses. Then the cut back to violent contact at 4:15 re-tightens. The man's stare at 4:10 is the human pivot of the section: an anonymous mass momentarily becomes one person looking at us. Mood: exhilarating but gruelling, collective rather than heroic.
