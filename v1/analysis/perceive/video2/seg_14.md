## What a viewer feels here and why

Effort, exposure and cold. The shaky, close, blurred shots (4:30-4:34) put you inside the struggle; the red hat, snow stuck to the jacket and the arms-behind-the-back lean convey the body bracing into the wind. The cut to an official holding up a skater (4:28) and the line of silent onlookers gives a sense of communal witnessing. The 4:23-4:25 shot of wind-streaked empty ice gives a pause, loneliness, scale. Loud, steady music keeps a feeling of relentless endurance rather than triumph. The cool blue final shot is quieter in mood and opens space after the claustrophobic close-ups. This is my interpretation of a human response; nothing here is on screen as text.
