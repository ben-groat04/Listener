# video2: the words (Public Service Broadcasting, "Elfstedentocht Part 2")

Provenance: the raw outputs committed to the repository are in `v1/data/video2/lyrics/`: `whisper_medium_mix_nl_transcribe`, `whisper_medium_mix_nl_translate`, `whisper_small_mix` and `whisper_medium_vocals_auto_transcribe` (txt and json). The faster-whisper medium and large-v3 re-runs on vocals-stem windows, with word probabilities, were scratch runs, not committed. Every rating below that rests on them is therefore at most Medium and marked "scratch run not committed". The committed vocals-stem run used automatic language detection and came out as Welsh (lang `cy`, probability 0.57) gibberish apart from the date and numbers, so it is no support for the Dutch wording.

Method: compared the three existing whisper transcripts (small on the mix, medium nl transcribe and translate on the mix), then re-ran faster-whisper (medium and large-v3, CPU int8, nl language hint, initial_prompt with Elfstedentocht/place names) on short windows of the demucs **vocals stem** (`stems_raw/htdemucs/song/vocals.wav`), which strips most of the music. Word-level timestamps and probabilities were checked. I cannot play audio; confidence reflects agreement between runs and word probabilities.

## 1. Summary

- All intelligible speech is **one Dutch archival voice-over, 0:41 to 2:36**: a short announcement about the 12th Elfstedentocht (18 January 1963). Nothing after 2:36 is intelligible speech.
- The whisper "words" after 3:20 (e.g. "In de lopen", "Vrijdiger", "End of the road", "Climbing up", "Een vreemde man", "Ik schaam me wel") are **hallucinations** on music (lead guitar/synth bleeding into the vocals stem). Different models/languages give unrelated words, probabilities are low and the text is gibberish ("sleutel, slaven, nemen, vormen, volsporten..."). Treat the last two-thirds as **instrumental, with no lyrics**. A repeated phrase ("Een vreemde man" x4, 4:06 to 4:16) had high word probability (0.6 to 0.97) but is semantically unrelated and almost certainly a rhythmic synth/guitar figure being "heard" as speech; not reported as words.
- Whisper transcribed `MUZIEK` at 0:28, 3:21, 4:16, 4:58: that is a subtitle-style hallucination meaning "music", not a word.

## 3. Who is speaking

- **One male-sounding narrator, in the style of Dutch newsreel/documentary voice-over** (OBSERVATION from transcript style and brief, formal sentences; I cannot hear his voice). The text is a **pre-race scene-setting announcement**: date, time of start, distance, weather, number and nationality of entrants. That is typical newsreel (Polygoon-like) or radio/TV commentary. INTERPRETATION only: research notes say the exact voice cannot be identified and the source is the Fries Film Archief; I do not attribute it to any named person.
- No interviewee, reporter dialogue, crowd chatter or singing was found by the transcripts. The band's contribution is entirely instrumental (hallucinated words after 3:20 are not sung words).
- **Recording era and quality:** consistent with a 1963 archival film soundtrack (the text itself is a period announcement in the present tense: "that is the Elfstedentocht"). Vocal energy per second (vocals stem) is strong in six clean blocks: 0:41-0:46, 0:51-0:55, 0:59-1:01 and 1:05-1:10 (loudest 1:08), 1:20-1:28, 1:49-1:56, 2:25-2:36. Between blocks, silence: the voice is cut and spaced around the music, not continuous. The modest probabilities on single words ("Stad zijn", "Canadezen") suggest band-limited, slightly muffled archival audio mixed under or over instrumentation, as expected for a mid-century optical/magnetic film track. This is inference; I did not view a spectrogram of the voice itself.
- Language: Dutch (not Frisian) with standard pronunciation as far as the transcripts show (the committed transcribe runs were forced to `nl`, so they give no independent language evidence; the committed auto-detect run on the vocals stem returned Welsh at 0.57, i.e. language detection on this stem is not informative). Frisian speech would be plausible for a Friesland event, but nothing was transcribed as such; unconfirmed.

## 4. Phrases with emotional or thematic weight

1. **"18 januari 1963" (0:41-0:45).** An opening date stamp: the film starts like a historical record; the date is the anchor for everything that follows, and the repeated or doubled reading marks it as the title card of the piece.
2. **"Een schaatsmarathon zonder weer(ga)" (0:51).** Names the event type and sets up its extraordinary status ("without equal"), or, taken literally, "without weather" (a bleak, almost deadpan phrase for a race held in a storm). Ambiguous; it is the thesis of the narrative either way.
3. **"Half zes in de ochtend, 200 kilometer door een Friesland waar tegen alleen de allersterkste zijn opgewassen" (0:59-1:10).** The emotional core: darkness, early hour, vast distance, and the claim that *only the strongest* can survive. It frames the ordeal and, in hindsight (under 1% finished, per research), is bleakly prophetic. Spoken slowly with pauses, so the line also acts as a musical hit point (interpretation).
4. **"Waar iedereen zich voor opmaakt, dat is de Elfstedentocht" (1:24-1:28).** The only communal, anticipatory line: "everyone is getting ready". It is the rhetorical peak of national excitement, and the only sentence where the name of the race is spoken twice in the whole transcript (1:21 and 1:28).
5. **"Temperatuur 16 graden beneden nul. Een monsterrit van ruim 200 kilometer" (1:49-1:56).** Cold plus scale: "monster" is the one strongly emotive word in the whole text, and it fits the later nickname "De Hel van '63".
6. **"575 wedstrijdrijders, rond 9500 toerrijders... Zweden, Canadezen, Zwitsers en Belgen" (2:25-2:36).** A list of numbers and nationalities: spoken dryly, it builds scale and the sense of a gathering crowd. The contrast between such a sober recital and the music (per other analysts' notes: calm, then building) carries the irony: nobody here knows how many will fail.

## 5. Caveats and reproducibility

- Whisper outputs are unreliable on gibberish stems; every transcription after 3:20 was discarded on purpose, and I say so rather than listing it as lyrics.
- Items marked "speculation" (zonder weerga, opgewassen, "[Alle] steden zijn [klaar]") should not be quoted as heard.
- Scripts and the windowed raw runs were in the session scratchpad and are not saved to the repo (scratch run not committed); settings: faster-whisper medium and large-v3, cpu, int8, beam 5, `condition_on_previous_text=False`, language `nl`, 16 kHz mono from librosa, with and without an initial prompt naming Elfstedentocht and likely nouns.
- Existing transcripts: `v1/data/video2/lyrics/whisper_*.txt`; stem: `v1/data/video2/stems_raw/htdemucs/song/vocals.wav`.
