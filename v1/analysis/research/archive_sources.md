## Candidate 1963 films in the FFAA (from the Elfstedentocht association site)

The official page says "All videos are from the Frisian Film & Audio Archive" and credits these 1963 items (H for existence, per fetch of https://elfstedentocht.frl/en/tour/the-tour-of-1963/):

| Title (as listed) | Credited maker | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Elfstedentocht 1963 | Rients Westra | no colour/sound info given |
| Elfstedentochten 1963 | Kleare Kimen | no colour/sound info given |
| Ice Marathon | Kleare Kimen: J. Foppema, Ir. D.P. Jax Mulder, F.P.A. (Frans) Wilming | listed twice |
| The Harsh 1963 Tour | Kleare Kimen: J.H. Zerb, W.L.C. Remy, J. Foppema, Ir. D.P. Jax Mulder, F.P.A. (Frans) Wilming | |
| Elfstedentocht 1963 (in colour) | P.J. Sturm | only item marked colour |

Kleare Kimen is an amateur film club (per search summary of the same page; M). The page gives no descriptions and no narrators.

Inference (M-L, mine): video2's colour footage is plausibly the P.J. Sturm colour film or a Kleare Kimen reel, and video1's black-and-white material (newspaper headlines, "Reportage ELFSTEDENTOCHT" banner, DOKKUM sign) plausibly another of these or a newsreel-style compilation. Nothing links a given video to a given film. Not confirmed.

## The "De Winter van 1963 in Friesland" intertitle

- Our own observation (video2 contact sheet_01): an intertitle reading "De Winter van 1963 in Friesland" on colour 16mm-style footage. This is a title card, so it is most consistent with an **amateur or club film**, not a newsreel (newsreels carry a studio logo, not a home-style title). Interpretation, M-L.
- Searching for that exact title returned no catalogue record (WebSearch, only unrelated hits such as the 2009 drama De Hel van '63). It may be a working title of one of the table items above, or a catalogue title not indexed online. The title is not on elfstedentocht.frl's list.

## Narrator and type of narration

- No source names any narrator for the footage PSB used. Searches for PSB interviews (Willgoose, Wrigglesworth, Explore the North, Omrop Fryslan 2013) returned nothing relevant. Searches for the narration phrase "een monsterrit van ruim tweehonderd kilometer" return only the MetaFilter post (whose title is that phrase, so the post author evidently heard it in the video).
- Style evidence (our own transcript, per artist_and_tracks.md): measured, factual Dutch, statistics (date, -16 degrees, over 200 km, 575 racers and about 9,500 tourists, nationalities), which reads as a **film commentary or newsreel-style voice-over**, not live radio (no live excitement, no place-by-place reporting heard). That is an interpretation, M-L. It is equally consistent with a club film that had a commentary recorded onto it, or a regional documentary such as "Dit is Friesland". An amateur silent film would normally not carry such narration, which slightly favours a produced regional/sponsor film or a newsreel for video1/video2's spoken parts. Unresolved.
- Han Hollander / Theo Koomen / Koos Postema: no evidence tying any to the audio. Postema is only the NTS studio presenter, a different broadcast.

## Corrections / additions to existing research files

1. artist_and_tracks.md line 25 says the "De Winter van 1963 in Friesland" title "suggests a Frisian amateur or regional film; not confirmed". Still true; add that the FFAA's 1963 list (Westra, Kleare Kimen, Sturm) and the colour "Dit is Friesland" item are the concrete candidates.
2. elfstedentocht_1963.md line 48 lists Westra, Kleare Kimen and Sturm as "amateur/other films" with high confidence. Fine, but note the Kleare Kimen titles "Ice Marathon" and "The Harsh 1963 Tour" are English-language titles listed on the English site (possibly translations of Dutch titles). Also note the site says all of them are from the FFAA.
3. elfstedentocht_1963.md line 50 and cultural_context.md line 23, 36 imply Polygoon/NTS-style narration as the working hypothesis. Evidence does not support Polygoon as PSB's source; PSB credit the Fries Film Archief only. Downgrade the Polygoon hypothesis to L.
4. cultural_context.md line 35 and context.md call video2 an "amateur reel". The intertitle supports "amateur or club film" only weakly; a sponsor/regional film ("Dit is Friesland", Esso) is also possible. Mark as unconfirmed.
5. The MetaFilter fact rests on a 403-blocked page seen only via search snippets by two independent searches; the writteninmusic.com article is the sturdier source for the commission.
6. Context note: the name changed; the archive is now Fries Film en Audio Archief (FFAA); "Fries Film Archief" is the older name used in 2013.
