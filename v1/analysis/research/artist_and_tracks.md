# Artist and tracks

## Verdict
Artist: Public Service Broadcasting (PSB, UK duo). Confidence: high on artist and project. Updated 2026-11-17 (wave 2): the Part 1 = video1 / Part 2 = video2 mapping is now HIGH (official lengths 3:09 / 5:04 from MusicBrainz; see part_mapping.md).
Work: "Elfstedentocht Part 1" (video1, 3:09) and "Elfstedentocht Part 2" (video2, file 5:06; official 5:04, the file has about 1.9 s of extra tail). A two-part commission about the Elfstedentocht of 18 Jan 1963, made for the Explore the North festival, Leeuwarden (Ljouwert).

## Evidence from the videos (own observation)
- Neither video has a title card or credits in the sampled sheets (video1 sheets 01, 10; video2 sheets 01, 16).
- video1 sheet_01: b/w archive; newspaper headlines "Leeuwarder Courant: Elfstedentocht op vrijdag" and "Friese Koerier: Elfstedentocht bovenmenselijk zwaar"; night crowds, bib numbers. sheet_10 (3:00-3:08): skater, "DOKKUM" sign, a banner reading "...Reportage ELFSTEDENTOCHT", then black. No video1 transcript exists in data (only video2/lyrics).
- video2 sheet_01: colour 16mm-style home-movie footage, intertitle "De Winter van 1963 in Friesland", hoar-frosted trees. sheet_16 (5:00-5:05): ice floes in the water, fade to black. This looks like the thaw/ending.
- video2 narration (whisper medium, Dutch, plus small): "18 januari 1963" (0:41), "Van het schaatsmarathon..." (0:50), "Temperatuur 16 graden beneden. Een monsterrit van ruim 200 kilometer" (1:49-1:56), "575 wedstrijdrijders, rond 9500 toerrijders, Noren, Zweden, Canadezen, Zwitsers en Belgen" (2:25-2:36, whisper garbled this). The 1963 date and the -16 C / 200 km claims are verified in the audio.
- video2 sung/chanted section 3:32-3:58: whisper-small English hits "Stuck", "End of the road", "Climbing up", "Stuck" (3:58). Whisper-medium in Dutch mode gives only noise ("Slop", "In de banken"), so these words are low-confidence ASR of a vocal/sample. Not independently confirmed from lyrics sources; I found none online.

(Updated 2026-11-17 (wave 2): 11 Nov 2013 is also the digital release date of the 'Elfstedentocht, Parts 1 & 2' release and Wikipedia calls it a Record Store Day single of that date; a 7" followed for Record Store Day 2014 (19 Apr 2014, M); one blog says the tracks first appeared in October 2013, so 'autumn 2013' is the safe wording.
