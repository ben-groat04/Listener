# Reception of "Elfstedentocht Part 1 / Part 2" (PSB, 2013)

Researcher: reception agent, 2026-11-17. Confidence: H high, M medium, L low. "Fetched" means the page was read; "snippet" means only a search summary was seen.

## Headline finding

Documented reception of these two tracks is thin. I found no Reddit threads, forum posts, YouTube comments, or interviews with J. Willgoose, Esq. specifically about them. Absence from a limited search is not proof that none exist. The material below is what I could verify. Do not treat it as a fan-reaction corpus.

## Commission and premiere

- The Frisian Explore The North festival asked PSB to "revive the hel van '63" for the 50th anniversary of the 1963 race. Footage and audio came from the Fries Film Archief. Source: https://writteninmusic.com/nieuws/public-service-broadcasting-doet-hel-van-63-herleven/ (fetched, 4 Nov 2013). H.
- The same article says PSB were booked to play Explore the North, Leeuwarden, on 23 Nov 2013, "with attention for the Elfstedentocht project", and that the deluxe *Inform - Educate - Entertain* was due 11 Nov 2013. The article does not call anything a "premiere" and gives no premiere date. It embeds both parts as videos. H for what it says; the premiere itself is unconfirmed (see gaps).
- Record Rewind Play (25 Dec 2013) says the track was made "in collaboration with the organisers of the Explore the North festival in Leeuwarden". https://www.recordrewindplay.co.uk/tracks/elfstedentocht-part-1/ (fetched). H.
- A Search-result summary of a Ticketmaster NL blog quotes the commission as made "op verzoek van festival 'Explore the North'... op basis van de beelden van de Elfstedentocht van 1963". https://blog.ticketmaster.nl/muziek/ruimte-public-service-broadcasting-937 (fetched; one sentence only). H.
- A search summary (not verified on the page itself) says the organisers approached the band in April 2013. Source given by search: the PSB store page https://store.publicservicebroadcasting.net/products/free-download-elfstedentocht-parts-one-and-two (returned 404 on fetch). L-M.

## Release history

- Digital release 11 Nov 2013 per the existing part_mapping.md (MusicBrainz) and Wikipedia's page for the band, which lists "Elfstedentocht" as a Record Store Day single dated 11 Nov 2013 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_Service_Broadcasting_(band), fetched). M: Wikipedia's wording is ambiguous.
- Limited-edition 7" for Record Store Day 2014; after fans said they could not get a copy or had no download code, PSB made both parts free downloads, "partly to say sorry to those who missed out". Blog post dated 27 May 2014, which says the tracks first appeared in October 2013. https://rwffmusic.blogspot.com/2014/05/track-of-day-public-service.html (fetched). M-H. Note "October 2013" conflicts mildly with the 11 Nov 2013 date; it may refer to the video upload or festival announcement.
- Free download page: https://store.publicservicebroadcasting.net/products/free-download-elfstedentocht-parts-one-and-two (404 now; known only from search listing).

## How listeners and critics describe the feeling

All quotations below are from the cited pages as fetched or summarised.

- "marrying a simple blood-pumping guitar riff to footage of the arm swinging heroics of Elfstedentocht skaters" ("worked their usual magic"). Record Rewind Play, Neil, 25 Dec 2013. https://www.recordrewindplay.co.uk/tracks/elfstedentocht-part-1/. Feeling: driving, physical, bodily effort. H.
- "An explosion of driving rhythm and gutsy guitars followed by a spell of glowing ambience." Track of the Day, 27 May 2014. https://rwffmusic.blogspot.com/2014/05/track-of-day-public-service.html. Feeling: surge, then release into warmth. This matches the Part 1 loud / Part 2 calmer split. H.
- Written In Music (4 Nov 2013): Part 1 "stevig" (solid, sturdy) and rock-oriented; Part 2 calmer, electronic. https://writteninmusic.com/nieuws/public-service-broadcasting-doet-hel-van-63-herleven/. H.
- KindaMuzik live review, Vera, Groningen, 23 Apr 2014 (published 26 Apr): the reviewer calls the two Elfstedentocht compositions "absolute highlights" of the concert, and says PSB "smeedt op enerverende wijze beeld en geluid tot een vloeiend geheel" (forge image and sound into a flowing whole in an exhilarating way). https://www.kindamuzik.net/live/public-service-broadcasting/public-service-broadcasting/24926/index.html (fetched; a second quote says that on record you increasingly long for accompanying images). H for the quote. Note this is a review of the live audio-visual show, not of the video online.
- Common thread: critics link the music to the physical effort and to the way picture and sound fuse. No source I found speaks of grief, nostalgia or hauntology; that reading would be this project's own interpretation, not reception.

## Are the songs played live?

- Yes, at least in 2014: KindaMuzik (above) says the two pieces were performed at Vera, Groningen, 23 Apr 2014. H.
- A podcast episode "Elfstedentocht" from Public Service Podcasting (25 Sep 2024, 35 min 29 s) says the tracks "may appear on the setlist for an upcoming tour" ten years on. https://uk-podcasts.co.uk/podcast/public-service-podcasting-1/elfstedentocht (fetched). M: it is a speculation by fan hosts, not a band statement. I did not verify whether they were played on the 2024-25 tour. setlist.fm search for "Elfstedentocht" returned no matches (https://www.setlist.fm/search?query=Public+Service+Broadcasting+Elfstedentocht, fetched), which is weak evidence only, because the search tool may not index song titles.
- Leeuwarden 23 Nov 2013: booked per Written In Music; I found no report of what was played.

## Corrections / additions to existing research

1. artist_and_tracks.md line 16 says the track is "included on the deluxe edition ... due 11 Nov". That is what the article says, but the 11 Nov 2013 date is also the digital release date of the "Parts 1 & 2" release; the two should not be conflated. Low severity.
2. part_mapping.md line 21 flags the AllMusic "Elfstedentocht" dated 19 April 2014 as unverified; it plausibly relates to the Record Store Day 2014 (19 Apr 2014) 7". I could not fetch AllMusic (403). L-M: Record Store Day 2014 was 19 April 2014 by my own knowledge, not from a fetched source here.
3. Release timing: one blog says October 2013, others 11 Nov 2013. Treat "autumn 2013" as safe.
4. The wording "premiere" in the brief is unconfirmed. Safer: "commissioned for, and booked to perform at, Explore the North, Leeuwarden, 23 Nov 2013".
