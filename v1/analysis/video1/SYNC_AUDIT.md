# Sync audit: video1 (Elfstedentocht Part 1)

Date 2026-11-17. Scope: every claim in REPORT.md, LISTENING.md, VOICES.md, FACTS.md, interpretation.md and v1/analysis/music/video1/music_reading.md that ties a picture change to a musical event, or says there is none.

## Method

- `v1/scripts/agents/dense_cuts.py video1` reads every frame at 25 fps (160 x 120), takes the mean frame difference and a 64-bin colour-histogram distance, and flags local peaks well above the neighbouring second. Output: `v1/data/video1/scenes/dense_cuts.json`. Each candidate has a tier: strong (histogram distance 0.12 or more, so new picture content), medium, or weak (often movement inside a shot; at least one weak or medium case, 2:55.1, was checked and is a false positive, as was 2:07.04).
- Result: 201 candidates. All 51 boundaries in shots.json are found. 143 candidates are not in shots.json, 94 of them strong or medium (79 strong). The old list therefore finds about a third of the cuts. The 12 to 42 s "shot" in shots.json holds about a dozen cuts.
- Spot checks (before and after frames pulled from the mp4 at the candidate time): confirmed real cuts at 0:03.48, 0:08.96, 0:16.56, 0:51.80, 1:05.48, 2:19.44, 2:25.64, 2:31.84, 2:48.16, 2:47.44 (whip blur), 1:39.40, 1:14.84, 0:31.88, 1:17.96, 1:27.16. False positive: 2:07.04 (same frame before and after) and 0:55.12.
- Sound events were timed on the stems at 5 ms (bass, drums) and on per_second_levels.json (vocals, 1 s bins; Whisper timings for words).
- Cut times are good to one frame (0.04 s). Cuts through a dark frame (1:55.0) are timed to the dark frame.

## Lessons

Sync claims need frames at several per second plus the stems at tens of milliseconds. A 1 fps still is a mid-second sample: it dropped three of four closing signs and a skater at 2:49, and a "correction" built on it was itself wrong. The dense list found the thump cut-pattern that every earlier pass dismissed.
