# t_birdwalk: the music, read blind from measurements

Blind mode: built only from `v1/data/t_birdwalk/` measurements and the team's window notes (`win_0000` to `win_0180`) and specialist files (rounds 1 to 3: form, harmony, rhythm, sound, voice, feeling, lineage). No context.md, research or web. I cannot hear; every "feels" is a reading tied to a number. British English, times m:ss.

**Evidence keys.** WIN = window notes; F3/H3/R3/S3/V3/FE3/L3 = round-3 specialist files; PSL = per_second_levels.json; CH = chords.json; LN = lead_notes.json; SUM = audio_features/summary.json; CLAP = clap_tags.json (weak). Confidence: high / medium / low.

---

## 1. The track in a paragraph

A 3:08 (188.15 s, SUM) programmed, bass-heavy rap track: one man talking rather than singing, over a single note (A) and a very simple loop world. There is no chord progression and no tune; the whole piece is a count of 12-second blocks (four bars at about 80 BPM felt; the tracker gives 152 BPM, so the half-time feel is interpretive) in which layers are taken away and put back. A near-sine, falling-glide sub-bass on A1 supplies roughly half the energy; a dry, close, mono voice delivers a boast about a dance called the "bird walk" (the transcript's phrase; wording from Whisper is medium confidence); a short plucked A layer and a sparse one-hit-every-1.55-s drum part sit around it. Loudness is flat within each state (about -11 to -14.5 dBFS in the groove sections, about -18.5 dBFS, 6-7 dB lower, in the three breaks), so the drama is spatial and bodily, not a rise in volume: the floor drops out (bass off, image opens wide), then the sub lands again and the picture snaps narrow. It feels cheeky, swaggering and playful over a heavy, slightly dark floor; not sad, not tender, with no arrival at the end: it is faded out. Overall mood is medium confidence; the structure is high confidence.

- **Drum hits one per about 1.55 s (high)** in the intro and breaks (e.g. 2:01.3, 2:02.9, 2:04.5 ... 2:10.7, WIN120). Window notes called this "one per bar" against the 152 grid; on the 80 BPM reading it is closer to a half-bar (see section 9).

## 5. Sound and production

- **Flat loudness, changing space (high).** Mix about -11 to -14.5 dBFS in groove sections and about -18.5 dBFS in the three breaks (0:47-0:59, 1:59-2:11, 2:23-2:35): flat within each state (PSL). The changes are the sub and the stereo image: width about 0.04-0.35 with bass on, 0.37-0.71 in breaks (PSL; e.g. 0:47-0:58, 2:00-2:10, 2:24-2:34). Cause: the bass and drums are mono (correlation 1.00), while the "other" bed is wide and decorrelated (0.0-0.15, S3); width follows the bass by energy ratio, not a mastering move (S3, high).
- **The sub (medium-high).** Near-mono, near-sine, a falling glide of about 0.7 semitone on A1, ringing about 1 s and gated; second harmonic about -28 dB; about 47 per cent of energy below 60 Hz (S3, F3). It sounds, on these measures, like a synthetic 808-type tone; identity is inference. Most of the weight is physical, not tonal.
- **The plucked layer (medium-low).** Short decay (0.12 s at 0:40-0:47, 0.26 s in the groove), falling harmonic ladder, a body at about 226 Hz plus a bright part near 900 Hz in the groove, a peak at 883 Hz in the 0:40 build (S3). "Pluck, square or vocal sample" cannot be settled. It is not a transposition of one sound (S3).
- **Dry and close (medium).** No long reverb tails: dark gaps between drum hits and syllables (-58 to -66 dB drum floor; gaps up to 31 dB at 2:42-2:58, WIN160). The voice stem shows crisp breaks.
- **Bandwidth (low on the exact number).** All windows see a hard top-end limit. Window notes put it at 6-7 kHz, 9-10 kHz or up to 16 kHz (see section 9); the safe statement is a band-limited, warm, boxy top.
- **Hook voice treatment (medium).** Hook voice width is in the voice itself (vocal-side tracks vocal-mid, r 0.56-0.97; does not track the "other" side channel, r about -0.1 to -0.2); verse voice is mono (side/mid 0.01-0.03) (S3, V3). Doubled, stereo-widened or stereo-recorded: cannot tell.
- **Production era (low-medium).** Falling-glide 808-like sub, sparse beat, dry voice: consistent with mid-2000s to 2010s Southern club rap (S3, L3); it does not separate crunk-era from trap-era.

## 7. The emotional journey and its musical causes

1. **0:00-0:12, grab and wait.** A close, loud voice (3-18 dB over the other layers), a single drum hit every 1.55 s, no bass at all (-70 dB). Cause: sparse drums plus absent low end create a held breath; the falling sweep from about 0:09 and the pickup hit at 0:11.6 point at the drop. Feeling: playful announcement, anticipation. (medium-high)
2. **0:12.4, impact.** Bass from -71 to about -19 dB in one second, mix +5 dB (-18 to -13 dBFS), sub-60 Hz share from about 1 per cent to 35 per cent (section measures, WIN0), image narrows. Feeling: a physical punch and a bouncing groove. (high that the event occurs; medium that it will be felt as described)
3. **0:24-0:47, hypnosis.** Voice locks to a near-monotone D4 while the image collapses to near-mono and drums thin by 10-20 dB (F3). Cause: no harmonic or level change, only repetition. Feeling: nod-along, a little claustrophobic. (medium)
4. **0:47.2, the floor drops.** Bass -14 to -71 dB, mix -7 dB, width 0.1 to 0.5-0.7. Feeling: sudden space and exposure, then 12 s of waiting. (high)
5. **0:59.3, the landing.** Hit, gap, bass; +5 dB; mono and heavy again. Feeling: release (high). After the first break the body has learnt the 12 s count, so every later cut and return is anticipated (FE3, medium).
6. **1:10.4, confidence.** The pitch rises about a fourth to a fifth (A2-C3 to G3), delivery becomes dense and dry, drum fills at 1:06-1:11. Feeling: someone stepping up to the mic (medium-high).
7. **1:23-1:33, a thin beam.** "Other" bed gone, width about 0, bass hits every 1.0-1.2 s, a lone A pedal. Feeling: tension, focus on the voice. 1:33-1:40: rising sweep, heavier drums, width returns, so lift and urgency (medium).
8. **1:41-1:47, drop and return inside the groove.** The bass stem dips from about -25 dB (1:40) to a floor of about -66 dB (1:43), with partial returns at 1:44-1:45, a flickering 5-6 s dip rather than a total gap, while drums and voice carry on; full return at 1:47. Feeling: a small stumble and slam (high on the measurement; the bass-hole reading in earlier rounds is medium for its exact extent, since F3 saw only fragments).
9. **1:34-2:00, euphoric weight.** Drums densest (about 4 per second), loudness flat. Cause: density, not tempo or harmony (R3, FE3). Feeling: relentless drive.
10. **1:59.3, a rush into a wall,** then deja vu in break 2; **2:11.3** another landing; **2:23.5** another cut, now with the hook voice and plucked stabs still walking through the empty space; the sweep at 2:32.5-2:35.5 is a riser; **2:35.5** return. Cause: the same subtraction template, repeated until it works as ritual (FE3).
11. **2:47.5-2:57, mechanical and eerie.** Voice vanishes; drums heavier from 2:50; bass still sounding to the fade; one-second 'other' spike at 2:47. Feeling: relief and unease at once (medium-low; interpretation).
12. **2:57-3:08, wry fall and withdrawal.** The falling gesture, then a 30 dB fade with no resolving chord or landing. Feeling: no arrival; the track is switched off, not finished (medium).

Mood in one line: bodily, boastful and playful over a dark floor; neither happy nor sad. Harmony supplies almost none of it (high); delivery, timbre and rhythm do (medium).

## 8. The five moments a listener remembers

1. **0:12.4, the first bass drop.** After 12 s with no low end, the sub enters (-71 to -19 dB) with a broad drum burst; the whole mix gets about 5 dB louder and narrower. It is the first and most clearly contrasted event. (high)
2. **0:47.2 to 0:59.3, the empty floor, and its return.** Bass leaves, the image blooms wide, only drum cracks and the chopped "bird walk" remain, then hit, gap, bass at 0:59.3. It teaches the listener the trick the rest of the track repeats. (high)
3. **The refrain, "that bird walk, watch me do that bird walk".** It is the only memorable "melody" (low, A2-C3, mostly spoken), heard at 0:12, 0:47-1:10 and 2:23-2:47; it is repeated and widened. (medium-high)
4. **2:23.5 to 2:35.5, the beatless hook.** The cut, the exposed wide voice with stabs on the 6.2 s figure, the riser at 2:32.5, then the slam back at 2:35.5. (medium-high)
5. **2:47.5 to 3:00, the voice-free stretch and the falling glide.** The voice abandons the track, the "other" layer and drums take over, and a wry falling gesture closes the vocal. It is the one place where the voice lets go. (medium; this is partly interpretation)

Near misses: the 1:41-1:47 bass hole, and the 1:34-2:00 dense stretch.

## 10. Uncertainties and what the measurements could not settle

- **Instrument identity.** Whether the bass is an 808, the "other" layer a pluck, synth or sampled voice, the drums programmed or played, cannot be known. CLAP's banjo/brass/guitar tags flip every 5 s and are not evidence.
- **Downbeat and bar length** (1.5 s or 3.0 s); kick versus snare versus clap.
- **Major or minor.** No sounding third in the instruments; the minor colour rests on the voice's C.
- **The words.** Whisper variations ("bank/back", "gummy/dummy/bummy", "worst day/your birthday", "burr-wop/bird walk"); the voice's lyrics after about 1:56 are not recoverable in the vocals pass; the meaning of the words beyond their surface (boast, dance) is not tested.
- Whether the voice-free stretch, the glide and the fade are an intended outro or an edit.
- **What produces the hook's width** (double, chorus, reverb, stereo recording), and whether the hook is pasted.
- **Verse length arithmetic.** The 1.2 s "anticipation" and loop counts are fitted, not read (F3, medium-low).
- **Lyrics after 1:56 and the 2:56.9 glide's nature** (voice, effect or sample).
- **Stem artefacts.** Demucs bleed (voice into "other", sub into the vocal stem: e.g. C#2 readings at 2:50-3:03 are leakage, not a note) limit every stem claim.
- **Era, region, artist, or purpose** (dance craze or not): not derivable; labels such as "dance-craze" remain medium-low interpretation.
- **Everything labelled "feels"** is an interpretation of numbers, not a measurement.
