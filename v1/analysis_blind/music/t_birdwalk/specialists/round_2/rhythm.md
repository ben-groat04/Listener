## 2. Answers to questions put to me

**FORM (Q1): do bass pulse and voice support a bar of 1.55 s (8-bar blocks) or the tracker's 0.39 s beat; is the 1.55 s hit a backbeat or a once-per-bar marker?**
The block durations are 12.0-12.2 s whatever you call the bar, so your block map stands; what changes is the label. Options: (a) 1.55 s bar at about 155 BPM (a hit per bar, 8-bar blocks) or (b) 3.1 s bar at about 78-80 BPM with the 1.55 s hit once per half-bar (4-bar blocks). I prefer (b), medium confidence, for three reasons. (i) The bass notes ring about 1.0 s (20 dB decay median 1.03 s, 10-90th percentile 0.87-2.67 s, 0:20-0:45 [R2b]); a bass at "155 BPM" would hold a quarter note of 0.39 s several times over, which is odd for single stabs, while at 80 BPM a 1.0 s note is a beat plus a third. (ii) Voice [V1] and drum hats both sit at 0.19 s (see 3), which is a sixteenth at 80 and an eighth at 155: both fit, so this does not discriminate. (iii) The "other" stem keeps a repeating figure through the 2:23.5-2:35.7 stop (see 4) whose repeat is **6.2 s** (146.30 to 152.53); at 80 BPM that is exactly two 3.1-3.0 s bars, at 155 BPM four 1.55 s bars. Both again fit. So honestly: bar length (1.5 s or 3.0 s) is **not decidable from timing**; what is decidable is the 16-beat (0.75 s beat) loop and the felt speed. Stay with half-time, because a head-nodding listener assigns a hit every 1.55 s to a slow backbeat/downbeat rather than a bar of fast four. The hit is "once per half-bar" in (b); it is a fair label to call it "the slow pulse".
