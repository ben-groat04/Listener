## 1. The track in a paragraph

An instrumental of 6:01 (360.98 s; measured, high) that begins with 11.3 s of exact digital silence, jolts into a bright, glitchy noise-and-click passage, settles into a dark near-mono A drone, then builds by one layer at a time: struck chordal stabs on a 4 s cycle (1:00), drums (1:04.8), bass (about 1:06), a quaver-driven bass and busy drums (1:53.4). From there it is a dense, loud, hypnotic drive at about 120 to 122 BPM over a fixed A centre (open fifth E, flat seven G, third never clearly heard), whose changes come from the bass and drums leaving and returning while the 1 s mix level stays within about 6 dB (-19.4 to -13.2 dBFS; about 3.6 dB at the 5th to 95th percentile) from 3:07 to 5:17. It ends by the drums leaving (5:17.1), the bass going (about 5:22.5), a held A-E pad, an uneven fade from about 5:29 (about 46 dB in 12 s, average 3.8 dB/s), and a one-sample cut to digital zero at 5:42.84 with 18.1 s of zero after. It feels stern, tense and trance-like rather than melodic or sad: the emotion comes from accumulation, thinning and re-grounding, not from tune, harmony or voice. No human voice is demonstrated anywhere (INFERRED, medium-high). Modern limited master (INFERRED, medium-high). Overall shape: measured, high; feeling words: interpretation.

Unresolved ending with no final hit (measured, high). Honourable mention: the 2:22 burst, the one place something "alive" shows against the wall (low-medium).
