## 1. One-paragraph verdict
INFERRED, confidence medium: this is an instrumental, guitar-band-shaped piece in the lineage of **post-rock / "cinematic rock" with a motorik-adjacent, krautrock-inflected pulse**, built as long repeated-then-escalating blocks over an A pedal, and framed by a **sample-collage prologue and an unplayed (rather than sung) fade-out**. Nothing in the measurements says dance music, hip-hop or metal. The sound that matters (wall of distorted-ish harmonic mid-range, a thin bass-led groove at about 123 BPM, no sung voice) sits closest to the Mogwai/Godspeed/Explosions-in-the-Sky/Neu!-descended family, with the narrow-band, noisy collage layers at the start and end pointing to a sampled-material tradition (found sound, broadcast-style fragments). Exact instruments, artists and dates are not recoverable (see end).
