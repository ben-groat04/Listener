## 2. Corrections to my own round 1
1. **Kick IOI (rule 5), corrected.** I reported kick-band median IOI 0.48-0.52 s "quarter-note kicks". The rhythm specialist measured median 0.26-0.29 s (eighths) and off-beat kicks after 2:20. My re-run, 40-120 Hz drum stem, IOI histograms (bins <0.15, 0.15-0.3, 0.3-0.42, 0.42-0.55, 0.55-0.7, 0.7-1.0, >1.0 s):
   - 2:20-3:20: 6, 44, 15, 23, 1, 29, 5 (n=125 onsets)
   - 3:20-4:20: 14, 59, 15, 34, 4, 17, 3 (n=148)
   - 4:20-5:17: 4, 15, 9, 28, 1, 27, 10 (n=95)
   The commonest spacing is 0.15-0.3 s (eighths at about 123 BPM), with a second peak at 0.42-0.55 s (quarters) and a third at 0.7-1.0 s (rests or half notes). So the kick band is not a clean quarter-note grid; my "kick sits on its own 0.49 s spacing" was selected spans and over-read. The kick band also catches bass and toms (separator bleed), so this is measured but of medium reliability. The 72-84 ms tracker-grid deviation I quoted therefore does not mean "loose kick"; I withdraw "played, not quantised" as a kick-based claim. The remaining evidence for "played or freely edited" is the bar-level autocorrelation near 0 (my round 1), the 4 s stabs that are not sample-identical (sound specialist: waveform correlation 0.17-0.33 against spectral cosine 0.91-0.94) and the tempo creep (rhythm). Tag: medium-low, not medium.
2. **Spoken sample at 4:28.9, lowered.** I tagged "speech, INFERRED high; sample, medium". New figure: vocals stem over 4:27-4:32 has a mean power of -82 dB (separator floor) against the mix at -23 dB; there is no vocal-stem energy that supports the Whisper sentence. Voice specialist and sound specialist both flag the same doubt. So the legible text comes only from a mix-pass transcript with no stem support: I lower this to INFERRED, low (could be a Whisper hallucination on a dense mix, or a very buried sample that the separator put in "other"). I no longer use it as lineage evidence for a "found-voice" tradition. The post-rock "speaking sample" comparison is withdrawn; what remains is "short voice-like traces used as texture" (2:22, 3:44-3:49, 5:30-5:42), which is weaker.
3. **Band-limited archive layer.** I wrote that narrow dark layers at 0:24-0:29 and 0:36-0:38 and the "all-band" collage might point to old source material. The sound specialist found a uniform lowpass cliff near 17 kHz in every section (lossy-codec-like) and no separate band-limited layer in the music proper; the harmony specialist's 10-25 cents flat tuning is the only age-like cue. I accept: those dark layers at 0:24-0:29 are simply the sparse A drone (low-pass because the content is low), not a lo-fi layer. Era judgement stays "modern production" but I raise it to medium-high (agreeing with sound, form, rhythm), because peak 0.0 dBFS with +0.50 dBTP (sound), 14-17 dB crest at -15 to -17 dBFS RMS and exact digital zeros at both ends all point the same way. The only band-limited material I can name is the 5:30-5:42 voice-like residue (below).
4. **Tempo.** I wrote one fixed "123 BPM, motorik". Rhythm's per-20 s autocorrelation shows about 117.5 BPM at 1:40-2:00 rising to about 126 by 5:00, and a 4 s block clock (8 beats at about 120 BPM) in 1:00-1:40. Correct: the motorik reference should read "about 120-126 BPM, drifting upwards", which fits a played or hand-mapped pulse rather than a fixed DAW clock (rhythm's INFERRED low-medium; I keep that tag). Motorik normally means a steady straight 4/4 beat; a slow six per cent push is a human-ensemble trait, still consistent with the lineage, but the exact "Neu!"-style metronomic lock is not shown. I lower "motorik" to "motorik-adjacent drive" (INFERRED, low-medium).

## 3. Answers to questions put to me, and links to other areas
(Questions 1-2 below came to the lineage role only indirectly; no specialist addressed me by name, so I answer the ones my area touches.)
- **Voice, Q3 (same source at 2:22, 4:28.9, 5:30?)**: my measured roll-offs. Vocals stem, 85%/95% roll-off: 2:21-2:27: 348 / 873 Hz (low, dark, power -38.8 dB); 3:44-3:49: 1.48 / 1.63 kHz (-63.6 dB, near floor); 5:30-5:42: 2.55 / 3.50 kHz (-59.7 dB); 4:27-4:32: 2.59 / 5.43 kHz at -82 dB (floor, i.e. nothing). One speech source is not supported. Mix roll-off at 5:30-5:42 is 681 Hz / 1.53 kHz, so the voice-like layer (95% 3.5 kHz) is brighter than the fading pad, not narrower than the mix; the voice specialist's "band-limited against full-band" cue therefore reads weakly (sound's reading, that the narrowing is only the pad fading, is right). I lower the "broadcast-like" idea to INFERRED, low.
- **Harmony, Q1/Q2 (mode, third, F and Dm)**: the harmony specialist's chroma (C 0.42, C# 0.37 against A = 1.0; strong A, E, G) agrees with my bass pitch-class counts (A 458, G 154, C about 20). I adopt their reading: A-centred, third not established, A minor-pentatonic/Mixolydian-leaning (INFERRED, medium). Lineage consequence: no third plus flat-seventh drone-riff is the stoner/krautrock/post-rock vocabulary, and not blues-rock (which would show the third bending between minor and major), pop or modal jazz. I also accept their correction that G is a bass root at times (bass chroma G 1.00 at 4:00-4:40): the 4:00-4:40 A/G swing is an I-bVII two-chord vamp, a classic rock/psychedelic cell (high that the cell is A/G; medium that the lineage label is right).
- **Rhythm, Q (tempo drift, 4 s clock)**: see correction 4. A 4 s (8-beat) cycle at 1:00-1:40 with a 25 dB, 3 s decay per stab (rhythm, sound) is a build-by-slow-pulse device: long-cycle, few-event openings are characteristic of post-rock and film-score cues, rather than rock groove music. This part of the lineage claim is unaffected by the kick correction.
- **Form (rhythm-section layering, not loudness)**: agreed with the form and sound specialists; the plateau of 2-4 dB loudness range from 2:00 to 5:15 means change is carried by stems entering and leaving (bass out 3:43.3 by about 40 dB, drums out 5:17-5:19). That is the "layering" form-language (post-rock, krautrock) and my strongest lineage evidence, now independent of the kick.
- **Feeling**: the tense/eerie colour (tense 0.46, sad 0.33 in CLAP, weak) is consistent with a drone-plus-vamp texture and a long dissolve; I link the feeling of "from another time or place" at 5:30-5:42 to the mid-band murmur and the 22 dB-plus fade (feeling specialist), but the lineage evidence says only "post-rock/ambient-style dissolve ending", not archive provenance.
- **Electronic vein**: sound's "other equals the mix to within 0.4 dB before 1:04" and the digital-zero prologue (0:00-0:11.30, then clicks with centroid about 3.8 kHz) keep my "glitch/found-sound prologue" reading, tag INFERRED, medium; it rests on measurement of the noise, not on any identification of its source.
