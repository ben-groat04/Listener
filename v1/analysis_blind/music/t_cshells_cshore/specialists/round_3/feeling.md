## 5. Settled conclusions
- The track is felt as one long, stern, hypnotic A-pedal build (open fifth, flat seven, third masked; medium), whose emotion comes from layers entering and leaving, not from tune, harmony or voice. High.
- Weight and lightness are carried by the bass stem: its exits and entries at 3:43.5, 4:11.5 and 4:36.5 make the "lightening" and "re-grounding", while the mix level stays within about 3 dB. High on mechanism.
- The groove is human and steady rather than machine-like: about 120 BPM, climbing to about 122 by 3:10 and then undirected; my round-2 "quickening to 126" is withdrawn. Medium-high for 1:40-3:10, low after.
- The ending is a fade out of hearing (3.2-3.7 dB/s, inaudible by about 5:39) then a cut at -70 dBFS that is not felt as a jolt; the "murmur" cannot be established as a voice. High on fade and cut, low on voice.
- Production is modern and compressed (0 dBFS peak, crest 14-17 dB), with no measured archive layer; the only "alive" flicker is the pitched 2:22 burst, which is not a repeating device. High on production, low-medium on the burst's nature.
