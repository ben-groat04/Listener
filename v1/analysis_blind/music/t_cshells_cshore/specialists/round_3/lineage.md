## 1. Answers to questions put to me
- **Rhythm (round 2, via my Q1): is the kick loose, or is the tracker phase off?** Rhythm could not separate them: the drum stem has a flat kick phase histogram on 35-110 Hz, and sound measured the drum stem's 45-90 Hz band at -48.6 dB, 17.6 dB below the bass stem (-31.0), with most energy at 90 Hz-3 kHz. My 40-120 Hz IOI histograms (modes 0.15-0.3 s, 0.42-0.55 s, 0.7-1.0 s) are therefore dominated by bass, toms and bleed. I accept: **no measured kick grid exists, so no kick-based sync or looseness claim is made by lineage.** The only reliable pulse is the bass quarter (0.506 s falling to 0.484 s) plus eighth-note mid-band drum hits. Tag: measured (medium) for the bass pulse; "motorik quarter-note kick" is withdrawn (low).
- **Sound Q on drum low end:** the absence of a deep drum band is itself lineage-relevant. A modern drum-machine or sampled kick (808-style, trance or house) normally gives a strong 45-90 Hz peak; its absence points away from electronic dance lineage and toward a live kit recorded or separated with a mid-heavy balance (INFERRED, low-medium; separator behaviour could hide it).
- **Harmony (modes, G, third):** adopted as in round 2. A pedal on A with E, flat seven (G), third undecided (C# ahead of C by only 4-6 dB, possibly harmonic of a saturated tone). The lineage reading is "modal rock vamp (I-bVII) with drone", not blues-rock, not jazz, not pop (INFERRED, medium). I do not claim major or minor.
- **Voice / Feeling / Form: the 4:28.9 "top of a mountain" text.** Agreed it is a hallucination or unsupported: vocals stem -82 dB (separator floor) against the mix at -23 dB, Whisper no_speech_prob 0.93, one 0.02 s segment. I stay at INFERRED, low that it is any sample, and I use no "found-voice" tradition as evidence. The voice events (2:22 two dry low-mid bursts, 5:12 a steady 1572 Hz tone, 5:30-5:42 quiet band-limited murmur, 4:29 nothing) are **not one recurring device** (sound, voice): lineage drops "spoken-sample post-rock" entirely.
- **Form, Q (is 3:44 a section start?):** from the lineage side, a bass dropout of about 40 dB at 3:43.3 with the mix moving only 0-4 dB, plus a brighter 1-4 kHz (+6.4 dB), is typical of subtractive layering (a krautrock/post-rock device: remove the floor, keep the pulse). It reads as a sub-section colour change inside a 3:20-4:40 block rather than a new verse-like section (INFERRED, medium; form owns the final call).
