## 5. Settled conclusions

INFERRED, medium-high.

- **Voice-like events are not a repeating device or a set of section markers:** four different spectral characters, 2 of 5 near boundaries, no repeat proven. INFERRED, medium.
- **No archive or band-limited voice layer:** whole mix 95 % rolloff about 3.1-3.5 kHz, so no fragment is narrower than the band; voice carries no era evidence, and the era judgement rests on mastering figures (modern master, medium-high).
- **The ending's closure is the fade and the cut, not a voice:** 40-48 dB decay then digital zero at 5:42.84; the murmur is 27 dB under the fading mix and only an uncertain texture.
