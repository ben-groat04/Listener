## 9. What a listener feels, and why
- **Settled, steady groove at 0:40-0:43** (pulse at 123 BPM, near-mono, dark centroid 315 Hz): grounded, slightly dour. Cause: strokes at a constant 0.49-0.5 s on F/Am/Dm over a held A.
- **Hollow, suspended calm at 0:43-0:47**: the strokes stop, the energy collapses to 110 Hz, 96% below 200 Hz, level -47 dBFS at the lowest. Feels like held breath or distance (metaphor); measured cause is the removal of onsets and loss of high partials.
- **Lift at 0:47.4-0:49**: +16 dB in 3 s with centroid up from 121 to 541 Hz; the return of the pulse feels like a sudden presence.
- **Unease at 0:49-0:54**: Dm / D flipping every 0.5 s (minor vs major third) over the A pedal gives a restless, never-settling harmony; the CLAP 'tense' label (0.95-1.0) agrees (weak).
- **A catch at 0:55**: the mix falls to -60.6 dBFS (28 dB down) for about a second, then slams back at -32.9: an expectation-and-hit. Then 0:56-1:00 a growing drum-ish layer and the D5/A#3 high note give urgency leading into the stabs of the next window.
