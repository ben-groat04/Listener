## How this window moves the song on
Compared with 2:40-3:00 (window_0160s), where bass was already a steady pulse and the mix was bright and busy with a drum drop-out near 2:55-3:00, this window starts tight and dry, speeds the bass up at 3:07, then at 3:13 turns darker and denser, with the drum texture continuous and the harmonic layer thinning briefly at 3:18. It is a gradual build towards the next window (3:20-3:40), where a rising swoop at about 3:27 and a bass drop-out (bass panel dark after 3:25) arrive. So this window is the build, and the following one is the release/contrast.

## What a listener feels, and why
- Drive and tension, early on: the steady, sharp quarter-note bass pulse at 123 BPM with silences between hits (3:00-3:07) is mechanical and propulsive.
- Rising urgency 3:07-3:13: faster bass, louder drums (+4 to +7 dB in stem RMS), brighter centroid (4142 Hz at 3:10) read as tightening and speeding up.
- A slightly hollowed, "heavier" mood after 3:13: darker top end and long bass notes, with an A/G pedal that never resolves to a clear major or minor third, make an unresolved, modal feeling (medium).
- A momentary lift-out at 3:18: "other" dropping 8-10 dB makes the groove feel stripped for 1-2 s.
- No human voice: the feeling is of machine-like instrumental momentum rather than a person speaking.
