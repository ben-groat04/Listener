## 8. How this window moves the song on

This window starts as full, bass-driven, drum-dense and pulse-led (200-204.5 s), then deliberately empties the bottom: bass out from about 205 s, a chord lift through Dm/D/Em, a rising 1.1 kHz glide at 207.5-208.5 s that ends in a drum gap, a long G (213-216.4 s), and the return of bass and a steady kick at 216.5-218 s into a held A. The window is a release-and-regroup middle section.
