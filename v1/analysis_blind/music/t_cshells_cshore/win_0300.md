## 1. In one paragraph

A drum-led, dense, clattering passage over a held A-minor-ish drone, with the bass first thinning out, then returning, and at the end the drums are the thing that leaves: from about 5:17.1 the drum stem decays to nothing by about 5:18.9 while bass and the "other" layer carry on at the same mix level. After a loud, drum-heavy stretch it opens into a drumless, texture-and-held-notes passage (continued in window_0320s.png). Two very short sung or spoken-like vocal events (about 5:12.3 to 5:12.8 and 5:16.7 to 5:17.2) sit on top. Confidence in the broad shape: high. Confidence in what instruments they are: low.
