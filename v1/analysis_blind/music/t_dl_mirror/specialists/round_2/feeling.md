## Not recoverable by measurement

- How any individual listener feels: these are responses the structure invites, not measured responses.
- The performer's intent or irony in the voice: stems give level, pitch and words only.
- Exact instrument identity and source (sample or live): spectra do not name sources.
- Recording date or whether any layer is archive: a shared 8 kHz ceiling reflects the file chain, not the source.
- Whether stutters and gaps are performed or DAW edits: the waveform shows the cut, not who made it.
- The true hook words and so the story meaning: Whisper varies per pass.
