## 1. What changes in my lineage reading

1. **"One-chord drone" is retracted for the hooks.** Round 1 said "a one-chord D-and-A drone bed" and "root-only bass" (carried from harmony). Harmony round 2 shows the bass walking G - F - C - D under each hook (four walks, about 3 s each) while the D+A bed holds, and the verses carry no walk. Corrected lineage: **verse = static drone over a D pedal bass; hook = the same drone plus a short bass walk iv - bIII - bVII - i shape (if D is tonic)**. That shape (a descending-by-step bass line over a pedal) is a modal-minor or Dorian-flavoured gesture used in dark or cinematic rap beats and in rock and funk vamps alike; it does not by itself pick a tradition. INFERRED, medium-low. My "dark minor-drone cinematic rap" tag stays at medium-low, but its description changes from "no real chord changes" to "no changes in verses, one repeated modal side-step in hooks".
2. **"Near-grid kick, sequencer-like" is weakened.** Round 1 leaned on a kick residual of 78 ms over 185 s (a colleague figure). Rhythm round 2 and sound round 2 show that a fixed global phase is held no better than random (39.5 ms against about 46 ms random; 43-45 ms), while intervals sit on the grid to about 20 ms. Result: the tempo is machine-steady (93.96-94.01 BPM, high), the hit positions are loop-like (waveform repeat r 0.75-0.85 at four bars) but the kick placement within the bar is not rigidly quantised across the file. My 32-step kick fold (below) shows the same: verse 1's two-bar pattern correlates 0.52 with verse 3's and negative with the hook and verse 2 folds under one fixed offset, which is the phase wander rhythm reported. I downgrade "DAW or drum-machine production, grid-locked" from INFERRED medium to **INFERRED low-medium**, reason: the grid statistic I leaned on does not survive re-measurement; the steady bar lag and the 4-bar bass-mute loop remain. A looser reading, "a looped, hand-triggered or humanised kit under a programmed arrangement", fits equally. The voice file reaches the same "programmed arrangement, performed part on top" reading (INFERRED, medium-low).
3. **Hat evidence: my "not trap" rested on a threshold-dependent count, and I now state its limit.** Round 1 counted 851 onsets in 5-7.8 kHz (4.6 per second) with 6 per cent under 0.1 s. Rhythm round 2 finds 75-83 per cent under 0.1 s with a 30 ms refractory in 6-9.5 kHz. So both of us are measuring detector settings. What survives is the **mid-band fold** (899 of 1,059 intervals at one sixteenth, 154 at two, straight positions, rhythm round 2) and the absence of any block that stands out as a roll section (rhythm round 2). Claim kept: not a trap-style hat pattern, INFERRED, **medium-low** (lowered from medium; a short roll cannot be excluded, and rhythm says the same).

## 7. Two new questions

1. **To sound:** the bass walk is a pitched tone stepping G1, F2 and D2 with D1/D2 at +15 to +18 cents. Is the bass tone at 37 Hz a sine-like fundamental with little energy above 100 Hz (a sub-bass voice) and does its 2nd harmonic at 74 Hz carry the pitch on small speakers? That would tell whether it is a synthesised sub or a sample, and whether the bass shares a source with the bed's 148 Hz stack (D1, D2, D3 are octaves of 37 Hz; 148 Hz is exactly 4 x 37 Hz).
2. **To form (with feeling):** if the hook is the only place the harmony moves, and each hook has two walks about 9 s apart under the same lyric lines, would you describe a hook as "two 4-bar phrases (statement and answer) plus a 4-bar answer without a walk", and is the 4-bar intro (drums and bed, bass in gaps) built from the verse's bass density (25 per cent) or the hook's? That decides whether the opening is a hook-first single-style arrangement or a verse-style intro that happens to carry a vocal chant.
