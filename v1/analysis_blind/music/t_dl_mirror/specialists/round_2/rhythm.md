## 5. Not recoverable by measurement

- **Drum source:** sampled break, drum machine or played kit; repeats and a 20 ms spread fit more than one.
- **Exact entered tempo:** I cover 94.0-94.3 BPM from autocorrelation and a drift fit, not a click.
- **Hit identity:** snare, clap or hat; I separated bands, not instruments. "Backbeat on 2 and 4" is the weakest claim.
- **Whether gaps are edits, gates or performance:** timing at the 80 ms level is at chance; intent is not measurable.
- **Absence of rolls anywhere:** my hat detector double-triggers, so a short roll could hide.
- **Whether bass gating is arrangement or a separation artefact:** the mix changes only 1-3 dB at most bass exits; the stem may share low end with the kick.
- **Source age, and whether the loop is archive material:** a 0.0 dBFS peak, a mono image and an 8-9 kHz roof can come from a lo-fi modern workflow.
- **Whether a listener hears 94 or half-time 47 BPM:** both are available; the measurements give the counting pulse, not the felt one.
