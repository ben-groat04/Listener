## 1. What changed since round 1 (headline)

1. **The four hooks are the same phrase pattern.** Aligned by cross-correlation (10 ms vocal-stem envelope, 10 s passes, lag search up to 6 s), eight hook passes from different hooks correlate at 0.90 to 0.96 (for example hook 1b with hook 4b 0.96, hook 2b with hook 4b 0.95, hook 2a with hook 3a 0.90). Pitch contours (pyin, semitones, 18 s) of hooks 2, 3 and 4 agree at r = 0.89 to 0.94 with an RMS difference of 0.9 to 1.1 semitones. Measured, high that the phrasing and tune are repeated. **But the waveform is not a pasted copy**: 1 s snippets aligned to 2 ms give waveform r of only 0.24 to 0.46 (hook 2 against hook 4) and -0.38 to 0.46 (hook 1 against hook 2); a duplicated take would give about 0.9 or more. So the hook is either re-sung to the same pattern or re-processed per pass; demucs bleed of the bed lowers waveform r, so I tag "not a pasted copy" INFERRED, medium-low. Hook 1's pitch contour agrees poorly with 2 to 4 (r 0.47 to 0.53, 3.9 semitones RMS) even though its gating pattern fits (envelope r 0.74 to 0.94 with hook 2); I cannot tell whether that is a real difference or a pyin octave slip at the entry (low).
2. **My round-1 hook start times for hooks 3 and 4 were late by about 2 s.** The vocal phrase pattern starts with a first 1 s hole at +0.2 to +0.8 s after the phrase starts. The hook starts that make the four hooks line up are about 0:10.4, 1:11.3, 2:12.0 and 3:13.1 (the first hole starts at 0:10.94, 1:11.89, 2:12.81, 3:13.33). Spacing 60.9, 60.7, 61.1 s. So the 3:13.8 to 3:15.09 "near-void" and the 2:13.3 gap are the **first hole of the hook pattern**, the same slot as 0:10.9 to 0:12.3, and the +11 dB return at 3:15.09 is the end of that hole, not a different kind of event. This agrees with my round-1 section table (hook 3 from 2:12, hook 4 from 3:13.5) and corrects the form file's 2:14.0 and 3:15.09 as hook starts (those are where the voice returns after the first hole). Measured on gaps (`voice_r2_tdl.py`), high for the 19 gaps; medium for the exact start (+/-0.3 s).
3. **The vocal gaps are very deep and short.** Nine gaps inside hooks measured: median stem level inside the gap -54 to -65 dB against -15 to -18 dB just before (35 to 50 dB drop); where the 5 ms envelope gave a clean fall, the 30 dB fall took 135 ms (0:10.94) and the rise 90 to 155 ms (0:12.29, 0:14.48, 0:24.83). No decay tail is visible. I discard a first-pass "floor" figure of -30 to -42 dB for later gaps: it was an indexing error in my own script, and the corrected medians (`voice_r2d` follow-up) show no trend across hooks. Measured, high for depth; medium for fall times (three clean cases only).

## 2. Answers to questions put to me

**Form (Q3): is the hook really sung, and is the last hook the same take?** Sung or chanted on pitch: INFERRED, high-medium. Reasons: stepwise tune (median 401 to 410 Hz in all four hooks, p90 444 to 445 Hz, max 554 to 594 Hz), and the contour repeats to about 1 semitone across hooks 2 to 4, which noise or random speech pitch would not do. Same take as hooks 1 to 3: the timing and tune repeat but the waveform does not (above), so "same phrase, not shown to be the same recording" (medium-low).

**Rhythm (Q3): do vocal onsets fall on the 94 BPM sixteenth grid, and where do the pull-outs sit against bar lines?** Grid: a local fixed 16th grid at 94.0 BPM (0.1596 s), phase fitted per 15 s window from drum-stem kick-band onsets (35 to 130 Hz), which is the kick grid; checked against the kick IOI by construction. The kick onsets themselves only concentrate to a mean R of 0.41 per window (R = 1 is perfect, about 0.14 is chance at n of about 40), so the grid is loosely defined. Verse vocal onsets (n about 70 per window, 12 windows 0:30 to 3:27): mean R = 0.24 against about 0.11 to 0.14 for random times; mean offset about -30 ms (the voice sits slightly ahead of the local kick grid, window range -78 to -6 ms). Reading: a **weak** pull of syllable onsets towards the kick grid, not a sync result (low confidence). Verse onset rate 5.0 to 5.2 per second against 6.27 sixteenths per second: about 0.8 of a sixteenth step per onset, so the rap is a free speech rhythm over a 16-step bar, not one syllable per grid step. Pull-out (gap) edges: 19 gap edges against the same local grid give a mean absolute deviation of about 44 ms against 40 ms for random times: **no preference** (low; resolution 10 ms, grid 160 ms). I cannot place the gaps against bar lines because the bar phase is not stable enough (round 1 rhythm: pattern shifts hits inside a two-bar frame). I can place them against the hook: first hole at +0.2 to +0.8 s, long hole (1.1 to 1.3 s) at +12.9 to +13.4 s after the hook start in all four hooks (0:23.56, 1:24.38, 2:25.35, 3:26.04), i.e. about 5.1 bars after the start at 2.554 s. Measured, medium-high. Hook 1 has one extra 1.04 s hole at +3.0 s (0:13.44) that the later hooks lack at that spot (hook 2 shows a 0.3 s gap at 1:22.8 instead): a small variation.

**Harmony (Q2): do the side-steps fall on the same words? Is the F a real sung note?** Yes for the words, checked on hooks 3 and 4 against the vocals-stem Whisper timestamps: the Gm-G-F run starts 2:17.4 and 2:28.8 (hook 3), 3:19.6 and 3:28.5 (hook 4), and the lines "In my period and in the morning" run 2:16.8 to 2:19.3 and 2:26.6 to 2:29.7 (hook 3); "In my theory and my morning" 3:18.0 to 3:20.1 and 3:28.1 to 3:30.4 (hook 4). So the chord excursion sits **under the "In my ___ in the morning" line both times, in both hooks** (side-step start +5.4 to +6.5 s and +15.2 to +16.8 s from the hook start, line at +4.5 and +14.3 s). Whisper timing is only good to about 0.5 s; hooks 1 and 2 I have not checked line by line (low). F against F#: pyin on the hook voice puts 15 to 17 % of voiced frames in the F4 band (335 to 365 Hz) and 3 to 6 % in the F#4 band (365 to 385 Hz) in every hook, which supports the harmony file's tally (F 17 %, F# 1 %); the same tally on a processed voice, so same limits (medium). The tune repeats to 1 semitone across hooks, so F is a chosen note, not noise (INFERRED, medium).

**Sound (Q3): is the vocal entry a sample or cut-in, and does hook versus verse differ in compression?** Entry at 0:10.4: +46 dB in one 0.25 s step; the stem is at -70 dB residue before and the hook gaps are as abrupt, so it is **an edit or gate on a vocal track** (the mix dips only 4 dB in other holes while drums and bed continue, per round 1). A sample or cut-in cannot be told from a gated live take (not recoverable). Dynamics (vocal stem, active frames only): verses crest factor (peak minus RMS) 14.6, 15.1, 15.1 dB, envelope p10 to p90 spread 20.3 to 20.9 dB; hooks 17.7 and 18.0 dB crest, spread 16.2 to 17.1 dB. Hook voice is about 4.5 dB quieter in RMS and about 3 dB peakier relative to it (peaks -2.9, -3.2 against -1.2 to -1.7 dBFS). That fits a continuously rapped verse sitting under a limiter or compressor, but a dense rap also just has a higher average (INFERRED, low). Double-tracking: not measurable from a mono, demucs-separated stem (not recoverable).

**Lineage (Q3): is the gating an edit, a gate, or a side-chained mute?** The vocal drops 35 to 50 dB in 0.1 to 0.15 s and the other three stems do not follow (mix dips 4 dB or less in most holes per round 1; the 3:13.8 hole to -22.5 dB is the exception, see section 3), so it is a **mute or gate on the voice channel**, not a mix-level side-chain. The hole positions repeat to within about 0.5 s in four hooks (+0.2 to +0.8 s and +12.9 to +13.4 s), which a gate triggered by a breath or by performance level would not do; a drawn mute, a re-triggered vocal chop or a hook phrase programmed in a DAW all fit. Breath versus producer's hand: the timing says arrangement (INFERRED, medium); a rehearsed performer could also repeat it (low). I withdraw nothing from round 1 here; I add that the gaps are not locked to the 16th grid.

**Feeling (Q3): is hook 4 higher or louder than hooks 1 to 3?** No. Active-frame RMS: hook 1 -19.3, hook 2 -20.0, hook 3 -19.9, hook 4 -20.2 dB. F0 median 401, 408, 403, 410 Hz; max 554, 570, 594, 594 Hz; fraction of voiced frames above 480 Hz 7.9, 8.4, 8.7 and **5.6 %** (hook 4 is the lowest). The D5 notes the feeling file saw at 3:16 are also present in hook 3 (594 Hz). So the climax feeling at 3:15 comes from the arrangement (the +11 dB mix return after the hole, bass and drums together; form and sound files), not from the voice getting higher or louder. Measured, high.
