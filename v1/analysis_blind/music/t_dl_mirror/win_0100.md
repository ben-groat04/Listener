## 7. Production-era cues (INFERRED)
- Sample peak 0.0 dBFS (48 samples at or above 0.999 FS in 20 s), RMS -10.5 dBFS, crest factor 10.5 dB, mix per-second -9 to -12 dBFS: loud, limited, modern-master level; a 1970s tape-sourced master would not normally sit this close to full scale. (INFERRED, medium-high)
- Loop regularity: the bass pattern repeats with a 10.2 s (4-bar) period at r = 0.76; drum onset envelope r = 0.54-0.65, not bit-identical, so a real (or humanised/programmed with variation) drum part against a tightly repeating bass/sample loop. (INFERRED, medium: points to a sample-based or sequenced production)
- Kick onset spread: against a fitted 16th grid at 93.98 BPM (period 0.1596 s) the kick onsets show an RMS deviation of **14.5 ms** (about 9 % of a 16th), with the on-beat hits within about 10-20 ms. That is a tight grid, tighter than live playing would normally show but looser than a pure quantised DAW (which would show about 1-5 ms on a stem onset detector; the stem separation and detector adds some of the 14.5 ms). (INFERRED, medium; not a strong discriminator)
- Band-limited layer: the "other" stem has centroid 1.27 kHz and sits almost entirely below ~2 kHz with nothing above ~8 kHz in the mix; this resembles a band-limited sample or an older-sounding source under a full-range drum/bass/vocal layer, though the vocal and drum stems carry much of the 2-8 kHz energy (PNG). The ambiguity: it could equally be a plain mellow synth. CLAP "vinyl crackle" 0.95 at 1:40 supports a worn-source texture but is a weak, noisy model. (INFERRED, low)
- Overall: reads as **modern, loud hip-hop production around a looped, sampled-sounding harmonic bed** rather than a single period-consistent recording. (INFERRED, medium)
- Sync statistics: none claimed (no cuts used).

## 8. How the window moves the song on
- 1:20-1:40 (PNG 0080s) was a more varied section: vocal gaps near 1:23-1:26 and ~1:29, melodic/sung lines up to MIDI ~73, chord labels Gm/F/Am at 1:27-1:30, a bass line more continuous. 1:40-2:00 is a **settling into groove**: static D bed, lowest voice range, a steady 10 s bass cycle (on 6 s, off 4 s). It is a plateau of the verse (the rapped story section), with energy held flat, not a build.
- Mild release/pressure alternation: each 10 s cycle the bass vanishes for 4 s (lightens the low end by ~2 dB, as the mix is almost unchanged) and returns, which is the only dynamic movement.
- 2:00-2:20 (PNG 0120s) continues the same cycle until ~2:11, then the vocal stem drops out for ~2 s at about 2:12.5-2:14 with a reverb-like smear, the pitch rises to MIDI 65-73 and the chords move to Gm: the first real change after this plateau. So 1:40-2:00 is the preparation.
