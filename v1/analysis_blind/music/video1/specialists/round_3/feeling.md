## 3. Connections

- **Rhythm to feeling:** a fixed 78.0 BPM, every-beat click, off-sixteenth thumps and a limp at steps 6 and 11 give a mechanical march; since the pulse never moves, every felt change is a removal (click gone 2:37, dropout 2:47.9, cut 3:08) or the single lunge (the 32nd roll at 3:06.25). That is why absence, not addition, produces my strongest feelings.
- **Sound to feeling:** weight = centred bass and sub; vastness = side-equal wall; closeness = dry mix. Loudness barely moves (-9.5 to -16.5 dBFS), so the listener is steered by weight and space.
- **Voice to feeling:** the narrow, centred, band-limited voice appears only in thin states and leaves at each gain edge (0:32, 1:27, 2:48; R3-voice). Felt: a human record, then overtaken by present energy. This is interpretation; the measured basis is band, placement and exits.
- **Harmony to feeling:** harmony stands still, so the emotion is stoic and modal (open D5, b7, no cadence). The only warm or wistful moments are the held A with B/C at 1:15.5-1:21 and the 2:13-2:38 alternation, where chords first move at bar level, which I hear as a lift (R3-harmony).
- **Form to feeling:** "subtract, then slam" (R3-form): bass out 1:15, brightness in 1:27, bass in 1:40 (+35 dB). Weight and brightness are never both on until 2:48, so the finale is felt as a long-delayed union.

## 5. Settled conclusions (5 bullets)

- **Character:** determined, mechanical, quietly heroic; endurance rather than celebration. Basis: constant 78.0 BPM, thirdless D5 with b7, no cadence, no sung tune (high on the measurements, medium on the feeling label).
- **The feeling is driven by weight and space, not volume or harmony:** bass presence, width and brightness make every strong edge (1:15, 1:27, 1:40, 2:13, 2:48) while loudness spans only about 7 dB (high).
- **Two peaks of different kinds:** 1:40-1:55 is pressure (loudest, 1:52, bass without width); 2:48-3:08 is arrival (widest, brightest, voice-free, wall plus centred bass, a plateau not a peak). Same pitches in both (medium-high).
- **Voice as human anchor:** a narrow, centred, old-sounding voice appears only in thin states and the band answers it, never over it; the felt arc is statement, route, then wordless answer (medium; interpretive).
- **Ending is unresolved:** a one-bar breath, then a lunge (32nd roll) and a hard simultaneous cut at 3:08.2-3:08.7, so the arrival is abruptly shut rather than concluded (medium-high).
