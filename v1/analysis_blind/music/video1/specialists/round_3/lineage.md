## 3. Connections

- **Form:** the layer-by-layer build and subtract, with drums and harmony nearly static, is the defining structural habit of post-rock and drone-rock, and of film or documentary scoring where a loop is varied by orchestration. The two climaxes (1:40 heaviest, 2:48 widest) are the "crescendo then second crescendo" shape.
- **Rhythm:** the missing backbeat is the strongest negative evidence: rock, motorik and stadium rock all have one. The pulse is closer to a programmed or processed kit (medium-low; stem leakage).
- **Harmony:** thirdless D5, b7, no V-I: riff and drone language, not pop or blues.
- **Sound:** dry, loud, clean to 18 kHz, a fixed 42.66 Hz sub: modern production, at least some sequenced element. Does not date the track beyond "late 1990s on".
- **Voice and feeling:** a narrow archival-style speech sample left alone by a wide wall: the documentary-score device; the feeling it gives (memory overtaken by present energy) is a reading, not a measurement.
