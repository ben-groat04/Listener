- **Voice:** list entries on bar downbeats about 0.3 beat late; the title sentence exactly on beats for 5 beats. My round 2 saw only the routine per-beat hit at its ends, Voice measured a level step (-21 to -14.8 dB at 0:27.9); I defer to Voice's numbers (low-medium).

## 6. Settled conclusions (final)

- **Tempo and metre:** 78.0 BPM, constant throughout (high); 4/4 with a bar of 3.08 s, 156 a plausible felt subdivision (medium); bar-one most likely at beat-position 2 of my fold (medium), supported independently by eight section edges, the held A at 1:15.5-1:18.7 and the voice's list entries.
- **Pattern:** an even click on every beat, low thumps on the off-sixteenths, holes at steps 6 and 11 (0:33-2:47), no backbeat, loop-like (bar similarity 0.81-0.85); closer to programmed or processed drums than a rock kit; a forward-leaning slog-with-a-lurch (medium-high).
- **Few, sharp pulse changes:** cell arrives 0:32; hi-filled swell 1:48-1:55 inside the loudest stretch; click absent about 2:37-2:42 then rebuilt; drum dropout of 1.5 beats at 2:47.9; and-a-pair cell to 3:06; 32nd-note roll then a simultaneous drum-and-bass cut at about 3:08.2 (high on each time).
- **Rhythm and form:** the form rides a fixed bar grid with layers entering on bar lines in 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8-bar blocks; the 8-beat sub and bass cycle at 2:01-2:38 keeps it; the drum cell rarely changes, so layers and space carry the structure and the drums mark only the hinges (0:32, 2:37-2:48, 3:06-3:08).
- **Rhythm and feeling:** a patient, mechanical, forward-leaning march (endurance, not dance); the rhythm's few events are removals (click gone at 2:37, dropout at 2:47.9, hard stop at 3:08.2) and one lunge (the roll), so its biggest effects come from absence.
