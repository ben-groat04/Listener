The nostalgic/triumphant tags from CLAP (nostalgic 0.95 to 1.0 at 0:45 and 0:55 to 1:00; triumphant 0.58 at 0:50) are weak evidence; they match a modal D with a flat seventh, which tends to sound open, wistful and heroic rather than sad. The listener hears factual information (date, temperature, distance) as a human voice from another time, which feels like archive material (inference; the "archive" feel is not measured, only the speech and its clipped, treated character).
- **A slow tightening / rising pressure** over 0:43 to 0:57. Cause: the "other" stem rising about 10 dB (from -24 to -14), the bass pulling back so the middle and top dominate, the mix peak at 0:57 to 0:58. The narrowing of stereo width at the peak (0.33 to 0.39) focuses the sound just as it is loudest. Confidence medium-high on the build, low on the narrowing as cause.
- **Static, hypnotic stability** from the D pedal with little harmonic change: the listener's attention is on texture and rhythm rather than chord movement. Confidence medium (the chord labels are noisy, but the chroma D row is on nearly constantly).
