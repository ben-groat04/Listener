- **Rhythm:** the 120 BPM lattice never moves except at the hole; sections are cut on its 2 s bars; the kick shifting from off-beat to every beat at the drop is the form's biggest single change, and it sits inside a silence so it is not heard as a shift but as an arrival.
- **Harmony:** form and harmony are one gesture. Loop A (14 bars) holds the tonic back; loop B (6 bars) gives it every 12 s; no modulation, so energy is made by other means.
- **Sound:** every edge has a mix signature (bass step, centred kick, top-band ramp, bass hole, hard edit); the brightness ramp is a slow control move across the cells.
