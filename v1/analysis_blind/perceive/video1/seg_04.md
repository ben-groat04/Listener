## 4. What a viewer feels here and why

Interpretation. A sense of a crowd-sized, cold and physical effort being gathered: restless, jostling, a bit breathless. The low, close, tilted, blurred camera (the "170" bib, the whipping legs, the pile of bodies at "460") puts us among the skaters, almost jostled, while the full-volume music pushes the energy up. Against it the stationary onlookers and the film-maker on his tripod give a watchful, documentary feel: the event is being observed and recorded (the cameraman at 1:00-1:04 and the note-taker at 1:18). The drop at 1:15 into a sparser, pulse-led sound with the hut and the notebook feels like arriving at an official stop: a checkpoint, a pause to be counted, tension eased but not resolved. The monochrome snow, with white sky merging into white ice, gives a cold, quiet, almost abstract look, and the vanishing of the voice for nearly 25 seconds lets the images speak.
