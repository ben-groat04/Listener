## 4. What a viewer feels here, and why
- Anticipation and crowd pressure (2:40-2:47): shoulder-to-shoulder crowds, jostling handheld camera, faces looking at something off-screen, an officer, a barrier. The loud, drum-led music and the repeated spoken refrain add to the sense of an event about to happen.
- A held breath at 2:48: the music thins and the "FINISH" banner appears, a sense of arrival and goal.
- Rush and strain (2:49-2:55): blur, dark frames, numbers on backs, then skaters bent low. Physical effort, hardship, anonymity, since we see backs and hoods rather than faces.
- Bleak beauty (2:53-2:59): blank white landscape, cracked ice, a newspaper that says "zwaar, zwaar, zwaar" ("heavy, heavy, heavy"). The headline and the dropouts line make the race feel brutal, and the cracked, wet ice suggests danger.
- Because the sound is steady and the pictures are old, there is also a nostalgic, documentary distance: these are real people from the past, treated as a collective rather than as individuals.
