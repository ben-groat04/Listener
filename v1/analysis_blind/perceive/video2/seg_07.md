## 4. What a viewer feels here and why

A cold, hypnotic, slightly eerie nostalgia: people small against a huge white plain, faded tints that feel like memory, and a loud, steady bass pulse that urges forward motion while the images are still. The white-out at 2:08 is a breath or blank: a moment of erasure before the landscape returns. The tinted pinks and blues feel dreamlike rather than documentary. Absent faces keep the people anonymous, a collective (a crowd on the ice) not individuals.
