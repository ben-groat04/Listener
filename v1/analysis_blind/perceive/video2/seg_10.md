## 4. What a viewer feels, and why

Interpretation: at the opening of the segment, cold, hushed beauty (lilac light, glassy ice, a long wide view) with a sense of watching something distant. When the skaters bear down on the lens and the shots shorten, tension and physical urgency take over: bodies bending, blur, bare numbers. Then the following shots make it intimate and a little claustrophobic: we are in the pack, seeing only backs and numbers. The dehumanising effect of numbers on backs, faces hidden, is striking; competitors are anonymous, enduring, collective. The darkening and cooling of the palette in the last seconds adds fatigue or fading light. The steady, bass-heavy music with no vocals fits: it is like effort and endurance rather than celebration.
