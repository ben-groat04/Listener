## 4. What a viewer feels, and why
Urgency, physical strain and a little danger. The close, shaking, low camera, whip-blur and faces at the lens put us inside the pack; loud, constant music at about 161 BPM supports a feeling of collective effort and being swept along. The bleached backlit passage (4:11-4:14) gives a brief breath: cold, pale, quiet-looking space, with bystanders in black coats watching, the effort shrunk to a small figure. Then back to close bodies, pushing shoulders. There is also a nostalgic, documentary warmth from the grain and old colour stock, and a sense of endurance: the sound does not relent and nobody smiles. Feeling is partly my interpretation.
