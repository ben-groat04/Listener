## 3. The thread I would develop: a picture that cuts just before the sound

Six of six close matches being early (0.09 to 0.30 s) is the pattern I would test next. If an editor cuts to a rhythm-section entry, cutting a hair early lets the new image register as the sound arrives, which is how a cut on the music is normally felt. If it were chance, the sign would be mixed; with matches of -0.09, -0.16, -0.10, -0.19, -0.12, -0.30 against one +0.48, the sign is skewed (a crude sign test on six same-signed values is p about 0.03, and the selection caveat again applies). The alternative, lag in the stem edges, would produce the same skew and is cheaper; I would test it by measuring the separator's edge lag on a known synthetic step. The pattern does not make the film synchronised to the beat; it makes it attentive to arrivals.
