## 2. Settled reading (five sentences)

This is a 6-minute instrumental trance track over a collage of old instructional and scientific film about looking, measuring and being watched, framed by a beach and a night pier. The music is a steady floor with changes made by adding and removing layers (drums, bass), not by melody, and has arrivals (1:53.4, 4:46.5) but no climax. The picture is not cut to the beat, but its hard cuts seem to track the larger layer entries at about a 0.1-0.3 s lead, which gives a faint sense that someone was listening. Meaning is open: I see recurring eyes, masks, alarms and apparatus, a local story at 3:20-3:39, and no stated thesis, and the shouting faces are alarm that I partly voiced myself. The mood is hushed, uneasy and wry, ending in a lamp-lit pier and 18 s of black and silence that I find affecting but cannot show was intended.

**The single moment that best captures the piece:** 1:53.3-1:53.4, after two dropouts and a fill, the groove locks in and the picture cuts to white lines converging on a face in profile, the one cut-to-edge match everyone, sceptics included, still accepts.
