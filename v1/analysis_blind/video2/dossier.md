## 1. Overview

A five-minute silent-era-looking home-movie reel is set to a single, machine-steady electronic piece. The picture is archival small-gauge colour cine film: grain, scratches, rounded frame corners, drifting colour tints, light leaks. It opens on a hand-lettered title board reading "DE WINTER VAN 1963 IN FRIESLAND" [SEEN, 0:00]. It then moves through frost, empty snowfields, a graveyard, roads and bridges, to a long middle of skaters on frozen water. The sequence runs from hush, through a build-up of people, a mass skating event and a pack race seen from inside the pack, to a quiet closing run of ice floes that fades to black. The music is in B minor at a constant 120 BPM, a loop machine with no chorus, tempo change or cadence [MUSIC]. It starts with 28 s of drumless pad, then a limping programmed groove. A low, flat Dutch-language narration (date, distance, temperature, entry numbers) rides the groove from 0:42 to 2:36. A drop at 1:28.6 turns the groove upright. A voice-free middle blooms in brightness, voice-like chops return at 3:28, and a plateau crests at 3:36 to 4:36. The music thins and then stops dead at 5:00.9. The key finding from the data is that the picture is **not locked to the beat** (sync.json: 37% of cuts within 80 ms of a beat against 43% by chance; Rayleigh p about 0.8). It is, however, **cut to the music's section changes**: the hard cuts at 1:28.6, 1:40.6, 2:36.6, 3:28.6 and 3:36.7 sit within about 0.1 s of the music's section edges (see 4). The narration says plainly what the pictures show; the feeling comes from the music and the picture. The faces are mostly withheld until the pack race, and the film's closing image is empty water and ice with no people.

### 2.1 Contradictions resolved

- **Tempo.** The older `perceive/video2/music.md` and every viewer note say about 161 BPM (an estimator value). The checked reading shows the tracker was reading the 0.375 s hi-hat cross-rhythm; the pulse is **120 BPM** and the groove is steady [MUSIC]. Viewer remarks about "half-time 80" are withdrawn.
- **Drums "absent" (viewers, seg 06 to 10, 12).** The stem separator put the kick into the bass. The mix has a drum machine at a near-constant level (drum stem about -19 dB from 1:40 to 4:50) [MUSIC]. Do not read "no drums" for 1:40 to 3:28.
- **Section letters (A, B, C) in the viewer notes** come from an auto-segmenter that does not agree with the checked song map. I use the song map; the viewers' boundaries at 2:37, 3:37 and 4:37 do agree with it (2:36.6, 3:36.6, 4:36.6).
- **Bass and loudness at the end.** Viewer seg 15 says the bass drops to zero at 4:49 and the loudest moment is at 4:48.1. The checked reading finds one bass stab per bar to 5:00.6 and the loudest 12 s at 4:12 to 4:24; the 4:48 "peak" is a per-second feature, not the measured top. I follow the checked reading.
- **Shots.json versus the picture.** (a) A hard cut at **1:28.6** is missing from the list (the list has 90.28 instead); my 20 fps frame difference gives a spike of 46.8 at 88.6 s, and the frame at 1:28 is the empty road while 1:29 is the bridge [SEEN]. (b) The list's cut at 69.2 s is not visible: the road shot runs to about 1:12 and ends in a cross-dissolve at 1:13 [SEEN 1:08 to 1:13; matches seg 04]. (c) Within the 8 s of fast bridge-and-quay material from 1:29 the list has too few cuts. The detector also over-splits colour-cycling shots (1:20, 1:43 to 1:50) and under-splits fast pack shots (4:00 to 4:20), as seg 05, 06 and 13 noted.
- **Faces.** The viewers' faces = 0 comes from the feature detector, which misses blurred and small faces. I see readable faces at 2:36 (a man in profile) and 4:10, 4:35 [SEEN]. The film mostly withholds faces, but not entirely.
- **Narration.** The language is Dutch (clear). Whisper's Welsh and English outputs are hallucinations. Exact clock time (half past five, six-thirty, or afternoon) and the last word of the 0:51 line remain uncertain.
- **Final stretch.** Seg 15 and 16 call the end a "fade out" of music; the checked reading shows a hard cut at 5:00.86 with three late hits. The picture fade is separate and later [SEEN].
- **Seg 13: "Fafn" and "Zeg/Hebs" strings** are Whisper artefacts on non-speech; ignore.
- **Seg 07 says the drum stem is "failed or absent"** while loud; see above.

**Reading.** In the sync data, beat phase and bar phase are random (Rayleigh p 0.82 and 0.78), and the correlation between picture motion and onsets is about zero. The edit keys to the macro form instead: the main hard cuts are put on the section edges, to the nearest bar. Within a section the picture goes its own way (colour flicker, fast pack cuts, whiteouts). Cut rate against loudness is weak (r about 0.23 to 0.27). Brightness and warmth do not follow the music (r about 0.03 and 0.2). One other coincidence: the 2:48 fill and 3:00 fill fall on the cuts into the skaters and into the long ice shot [NOTE+MUSIC], at the 12 s spacing of the music's cell. I did not test these against the 2 s lattice.

**Feeling arc, picture against music.** The first half is stillness and absence (no people until 0:53), carried by a pad and a limping groove; narration names the event over images that do not show it. The drop at 1:28.6 brings the crowd. The middle (1:40-3:28) shows skaters and facts as a collective, over a flat, locked groove. When the voice leaves (2:36), the film cuts to a lone car and frost; the music grows without people. From 3:28, close following shots with bibs and chopped voices; 3:36 to 4:36 puts the viewer inside the pack, at the loudest, brightest point of the music. The outro releases pressure (the long lane), and the end is ice floes, water and black: emptiness, not a finish line.
