## Identification
- **Artist:** Public Service Broadcasting (PSB), London duo (J. Willgoose, Esq.; Wrigglesworth). Confidence: high.
- **Track:** "Elfstedentocht Part 2" (Part 2). Mapping video2 = Part 2: high (official lengths, MusicBrainz release "Elfstedentocht, Parts 1 & 2": https://musicbrainz.org/release/f75e4a31-0aba-4d52-ab29-0a40df0bfabe).
- **Official duration:** 5:04 (304 s). **Our file:** 305.9 s; about 1.9 s over official, plausibly trailing fade to black. youtu.be/vrPBTwwnpdc is the official 'Elfstedentocht Part Two' (PSBHQ); not confirmed identical to our file.
- **Release:** digital 11 Nov 2013 (also described as a Record Store Day single; 7" for Record Store Day 2014; both parts later a free download). Safe wording: autumn 2013. The tracks are also linked to the deluxe *Inform - Educate - Entertain* (parent album 6 May 2013, Test Card Recordings). Medium confidence on format details.
- **Commission:** by the Explore the North festival, Leeuwarden, for the 50th anniversary of the 1963 Elfstedentocht (race of 18 Jan 1963, "De Hel van '63"). PSB were booked to play the festival on 23 Nov 2013. No confirmed "premiere". Source: writteninmusic.com, 4 Nov 2013 (high).
- **Archive source:** Fries Film Archief (now Fries Film en Audio Archief, FFAA), footage and voice-over. Which films, and who the narrator is: NOT confirmed. Polygoon/NTS newsreels are not shown to be used (low).
